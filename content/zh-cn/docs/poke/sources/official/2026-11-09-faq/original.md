Poke FAQs

## Product

### Which email clients can I connect to Poke?

Poke offers full support for Gmail and Outlook inboxes, calendars, and contact sync.

We have no immediate plans to support other email clients like iCloud or Yahoo Mail, but we are looking into these for the future.

### What integrations does Poke support?

We offer integrations with Notion, GitHub, Linear, and more - you can browse them all in our [recipe directory](https://poke.com/recipes?category=integrate). We're always adding new ones, though, so if there's something you want us to add, let us know at [poke@interaction.co](mailto:poke@interaction.co)!

If you're feeling ambitious, you can also build and add custom integrations using MCP. Check out our [docs](https://poke.com/docs/managing-integrations) to learn more.

### Can Poke build me a website?

Yes. Just ask Poke to build you one and it will design, build, and publish a site live at your handle: **yourname.poke.site**. Whether it's a personal site, a portfolio, a landing page, or a link in bio, describe what you want and Poke takes care of the rest.

You'll need to claim your handle first at [poke.com/claim](https://poke.com/claim). After that, ask Poke to make changes any time and it will redeploy your site.

## Account & Billing

### How much does Poke cost?

Most features in Poke are free. When you want more, Poke Pro is $19/month, and Poke Ultra is $199/month for frontier intelligence powering every action, with pay-as-you-go usage on top. Just ask Poke to help you upgrade. If you have any issues or are unable to upgrade, please email us at [poke@interaction.co](mailto:poke@interaction.co).

### How do I delete my account?

We're really sorry to see you go. If you'd like to delete your account, head to [poke.com/settings/privacy](https://poke.com/settings/privacy) and click "Delete Account." The full deletion process may take up to 24 hours.

Removal from backup archives may not be immediate, and legal holds can extend retention in some cases.

### How do I add/change a phone number?

We don't support multiple phone numbers yet. If you need to change yours, email us at [poke@interaction.co](mailto:poke@interaction.co) with:

(1) Your current phone number, (2) the new number you want, and (3) the email you used to sign up.

We'll take care of the rest!

### How do I change where Poke texts me?

Head to [poke.com/settings/messaging](https://poke.com/settings/messaging) and pick your preferred channel. Just know that Poke might text you from different numbers depending on which platform you choose.

## Poke Human

### What is Poke Human?

Poke Human dispatches real-world tasks to a real person on your behalf. Restaurant reservations, phone calls, placing orders, booking rides - anything that needs a human in the loop. Just ask Poke and it handles the rest.

### How much does it cost?

Poke Human is included with [Poke Ultra](https://poke.com/pricing?ultra=) ($199/month). Any real-world expenses the operator pays on your behalf (like your Uber or coffee order) are charged at cost - no markup - and deducted from your credit balance.

### Can the human see my data?

No. Operators only see the specific details needed for your task - like the restaurant name and party size. They cannot access your chat history, memories, or integrations.
