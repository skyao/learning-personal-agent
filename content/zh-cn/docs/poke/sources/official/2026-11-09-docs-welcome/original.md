Welcome to Poke

[**Poke**](https://poke.com) is your AI assistant that lives in Apple Messages, Telegram, WhatsApp, and RCS. Just chat naturally to manage your email, schedule meetings, set reminders, search the web, and more.

Built by [The Interaction Company of California](https://x.com/interaction).

Poke is now available in all regions worldwide, but please reach out to us at [poke@interaction.co](mailto:poke@interaction.co) if you have any trouble signing up or logging in.

## What You Can Do

Poke comes ready to help with:

- **Email Management** - Read, search, and draft emails without leaving your messages
- **Calendar** - Schedule meetings and check your availability
- **Reminders** - Set and manage reminders naturally
- **Web Search** - Get information from the internet
- **Integrations** - Connect your favorite apps and services to do even more

## Getting Started

### Start chatting

Simply message Poke through Apple Messages, Telegram, WhatsApp, or RCS. Ask it to check your email, add a calendar event, or search for something. [Sign up here](https://poke.com) if you haven't already.

### Add integrations

Browse and connect integrations to extend Poke with new capabilities from your favorite services, like creating and managing tasks or retrieving information.

[Managing Integrations](https://poke.com/docs/managing-integrations) - Learn how to connect apps and services
