Get started with Autopilot (Frontier)

[This article is prerelease documentation and is subject to change.]

Important

- Access to Autopilot (Frontier) requires the applicable **[Frontier preview program](https://adoption.microsoft.com/copilot/frontier-program/)** enrollment, preview assignment, and participation terms. Frontier enrollment alone doesn't enable every preview feature. Frontier previews are subject to the applicable preview terms of your customer agreements, and their availability and capabilities may change.
- This feature is in preview.
- Preview features may have restricted functionality and may not be released for general availability. These features are available before an official release so that customers can get early access and provide feedback.
- For more information, go to our [Microsoft Product Terms](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms/product/ForOnlineServices/MCA).

This article walks you through opening Autopilot, creating your Autopilot, and running your first task.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

- A Microsoft 365 work or school account associated with your organization. Personal Microsoft accounts aren't supported.
- Microsoft Copilot access - An active [Microsoft Copilot license](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/microsoft-365-copilot-licensing) assigned to your account.
- Frontier preview enrollment - Autopilot is available through the [Frontier program](https://adoption.microsoft.com/copilot/frontier-program/). Autopilot is off by default. Your organization's administrator licenses Autopilot, covers you with a spending policy for usage-based billing, and activates Autopilot for you.

Confirm the additional eligibility and task-specific access in [Autopilot requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/autopilot/requirements).

## Open Autopilot

1. Sign in at [copilot.cloud.microsoft](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft) or open the Microsoft Copilot desktop app with your work or school account.
2. Select **Home**, and then select Autopilot in the left navigation. After setup, this entry shows your Autopilot's name.

If Autopilot doesn't appear in the navigation, ask your administrator to check your access. Frontier enrollment alone doesn't make Autopilot available; your administrator also licenses it, covers you with a spending policy, and activates it.

## Create your Autopilot

You can have one Autopilot. If you don't have one yet, select **Create your Autopilot** and follow the prompts.

1. Choose a name and appearance. Coworkers see them in Microsoft Copilot, Microsoft Teams, and Outlook.
2. Review the suggested starter routines. Use the check boxes to accept or reject them.
3. Autopilot will then automatically provision.

Provisioning can take 10 to 30 minutes. You can leave the page while it finishes. You receive an email and a Teams notification when your Autopilot is ready.

When it's ready, review the people, responsibilities, and recurring commitments Autopilot inferred from your recent work. Treat the profile as a first pass. Select **Looks good** to accept it, or revise or reset anything that's inaccurate before you rely on it.

After setup, your Autopilot's name appears on the **Home** tab in the left navigation. Select its name to open your main conversation. To review its work or settings, expand the management panel in the upper-right corner.

## Where you can work with Autopilot

You can work with Autopilot in these places:

- **Microsoft Copilot** - On the web or in the desktop app, select your Autopilot's name on the **Home** tab in the left navigation.
- **Microsoft Teams** - Autopilot uses a private one-on-one chat with you for updates, questions, and decisions that need your context or approval.
- **Mobile** - On iOS and Android, use the Microsoft 365 Copilot, Teams, or Outlook app to check on progress, add instructions, or change direction.

## Run your first task

Start with a bounded request that doesn't send messages or change shared information.

1. Select your Autopilot's name in the left navigation to open your main conversation.
2. In the composer, describe the outcome, relevant sources, and limits. For example, "Summarize this week's launch discussion and draft an agenda for the review. Don't send messages or change calendar events."
3. Submit the message using the send control.
4. Answer any clarifying questions. Review requested actions and approvals before responding.
5. Check the result against its sources. If you requested a saved file, open the final link and verify its location and access.

Autopilot doesn't ask you to approve every step. Actions that match your request can run without an approval card; others, and all payments, wait for you to select **Allow** or **Deny**. For details, see [Set permissions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/autopilot/approval#set-permissions).

## Review activity and settings

Select the **Activity** tab. Use **Working now** to find work in progress and **Needs your input** to find requests for your attention.

Select the **Settings** tab to review your Autopilot’s name, appearance, memory, and computers. Select your Autopilot's name to change its name and appearance.
