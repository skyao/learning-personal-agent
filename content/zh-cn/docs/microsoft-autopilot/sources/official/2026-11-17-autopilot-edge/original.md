Work in your Microsoft Edge browser

[This article is prerelease documentation and is subject to change.]

Important

- Access to Autopilot (Frontier) requires the applicable **[Frontier preview program](https://adoption.microsoft.com/copilot/frontier-program/)** enrollment, preview assignment, and participation terms. Frontier enrollment alone doesn't enable every preview feature. Frontier previews are subject to the applicable preview terms of your customer agreements, and their availability and capabilities may change.
- This feature is in preview.
- Preview features may have restricted functionality and may not be released for general availability. These features are available before an official release so that customers can get early access and provide feedback.
- For more information, go to our [Microsoft Product Terms](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms/product/ForOnlineServices/MCA).

Where your organization enables it, Autopilot can work in Microsoft Edge on your device, using the sites you're already signed in to. For example, you can ask it to look up information on a site that it can't
otherwise reach, or to fill in a web form for you to review.

Note

Browser use is rolling out during the preview and might not be available to you.

Before Autopilot uses your browser:

- Microsoft Edge must be open on your device, signed in with your work account, and connected to Autopilot. If Edge isn't connected, Autopilot can't use your browser.
- You turn on browser use the first time. The prompt explains what Autopilot can reach and do.
- The request must come from you in your private conversation, either in Microsoft Copilot or in your one-on-one Teams chat. Routines don't use your browser.

When it works in your browser, Autopilot:

- Opens, reads, and acts only in the tabs it works with for that conversation. It doesn't see your other tabs.
- Asks for your approval before sensitive actions, such as changing an account, checking out or paying, entering card information, permanently deleting something, or submitting personal information or a message to an external site.
- Sends the page content it reads to its cloud model to complete the task. Your passwords and cookies stay in Edge, but information shown on signed-in pages can be part of what it reads.

Your organization's policies, Edge policies, and site rules can still block an action. Check the result on the site before relying on it.

Autopilot can't access local folders or apps on your device.
