## What is agentic BFCM?

BFCM is the four days from Black Friday to Cyber Monday, the busiest stretch of the year for online stores. Agentic BFCM is the share of it that AI agents handle themselves: orders an agent such as Meta's Muse, ChatGPT or Grok completes on the shopper's behalf, from finding the product to paying for it.

That's a narrower measure than the AI numbers most holiday reports quote. AI-referred traffic counts shoppers who click through from an AI assistant, and AI-influenced sales count purchases AI played any part in; an agentic order is one the agent placed. The [Black Friday and Cyber Monday 2026 statistics](/black-friday-cyber-monday-statistics) put the published forecasts for all three side by side; the numbers below are the orders agents placed, counted across Forter's network.

## How AI agents check out

Agents buy in one of two ways. Protocol agents, ChatGPT and Google, check out through commerce protocols (OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol and Google's Universal Commerce Protocol), where the store hands the agent its checkout. Scraper agents, Muse, Instinct, Grok and ChatGPT Agent, drive the store's own pages like a shopper would: browse, add to cart, fill in the forms and pay.

In the week of Nov 16, 2026, 80% of agentic orders on Forter's network went through the store's own pages and 20% through a protocol.

In the week of Aug 31, 2026, the store's pages had 12%.

[ACP vs UCP vs AP2](/agentic-commerce-protocols) compares the protocols.

## Getting a store ready for agentic BFCM

1. **Don't block agents with the bots.** Scraper agents look like the automation that bot rules stop, so blanket challenges on automated browsers turn their customers away on the busiest days of the year. Judge the identity and intent behind the order instead ([AI agent fraud](/ai-agent-fraud)).
2. **Recognize agents that sign their requests.** Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol ([Visa](https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.21716.html)) and Web Bot Auth ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/web-bot-auth/)) let an agent prove who it is on every request.
3. **Make the store readable to agents.** Agents pick products from what they can read: structured product data, prices and stock they can parse, a checkout they can complete. The free [AI readiness check](/ai-readiness) scans a store for what agents need.
4. **Offer protocol checkout where you can.** Protocol agents can only buy where the store supports their protocol ([ACP vs UCP vs AP2](/agentic-commerce-protocols)).
5. **Expect fraud to automate too.** The season's fraud rings use the same tools; [Black Friday fraud](/black-friday-fraud) covers the attacks that peak over Cyber Week and how to prevent them.

### Should stores block AI agents during BFCM?

Not as a group: an agent acting for a real customer is a sale, and blanket bot rules turn it away. Judge the identity and intent behind each order, recognize agents that sign their requests, such as those using Visa’s Trusted Agent Protocol, and keep stopping the bots and fraud rings that automate too.

### How do AI shopping agents check out?

Two ways: protocol agents such as ChatGPT and Google check out through commerce protocols the store supports, and scraper agents such as Muse, Instinct and Grok fill in the store’s own checkout pages like a shopper would.

### Is agentic BFCM the same as AI traffic on Black Friday?

No. AI-referred traffic counts shoppers who click through to a store from an AI assistant, and AI-influenced sales count purchases AI played any part in. Agentic orders are the ones an AI agent completes itself, which is what Forter counts.

## About the data

Orders Forter's network identified as placed by AI agents, aggregated and anonymized across Forter's global network of merchants and refreshed daily. Shares are of agentic orders, or of agentic spend, over the latest 90 days the data has (Aug 28 – Nov 25, 2026) unless a sentence says the last 7 days; order values are in US dollars.

Protocol agents (ChatGPT and Google) check out through commerce protocols and are identified by where the shopper came from; scraper agents (Muse, Instinct, Grok and ChatGPT Agent) drive the store's own checkout pages and are identified by the agent platform. ChatGPT Agent is counted apart from ChatGPT's protocol checkout, and "all agents" counts each agentic order once. Instinct and Grok are counted from September 1, 2026 and Muse from September 21, the days Forter started detecting them reliably, which may be later than they started placing orders; growth since the first week includes them arriving.

Peak Season's numbers compare this season with the same days of 2025, weekday for weekday, from Forter's global network of merchants, aggregated and anonymized and refreshed every hour.
