## What counts as an AI shopping agent

An AI assistant that suggests a product and links to a store sends a referral: the shopper still clicks through and buys. An AI shopping agent goes further. It adds the item to the cart, fills in the shipping and payment details and places the order on the shopper's behalf, usually after asking them to approve it. This guide counts those orders, the ones Forter's network identifies as placed by an agent. Other assistants help people shop too, among them Amazon's Rufus, Perplexity and Microsoft's Copilot; the agents ranked here are the ones Forter's data breaks out.

## Scrapers vs protocols: how agents check out

** Scraper agents** shop the way a person does: they load the store's pages, fill in its forms and click through its checkout. That works on any store, but it's slower, and it breaks when a page changes. ** Protocol agents** check out through a commerce protocol (ACP or UCP), so the order goes straight to the store's systems; they can only buy where the store has integrated the protocol.

Over the latest 90 days (Aug 28 – Nov 25, 2026), scraper agents placed 70% of agentic orders and protocol agents 30%.

In the latest full week, from Nov 16, 2026, scraper agents placed 80%.

## What this means for your store

- ** Most agent orders come through your own pages.** Scraper agents need product pages, prices, stock and checkout steps they can read and complete, and they get stuck when a page changes or a challenge stops every automated browser.
- ** Integrate the protocols where your shoppers' agents are.** ChatGPT's commerce works through ACP, and Google's checkout in AI Mode and Gemini through UCP.
- ** Tell an agent from a fraudster by who is behind it.** An agent acts for a real customer, with a real identity and payment method: blocking all automation turns those customers away, and letting all of it through lets fraud in. [AI agent fraud](https://ai.forter.com/ai-agent-fraud) has how.

## Questions and answers

### Can AI agents buy things for you?

Yes. Agents such as Meta’s Muse, Grok, ChatGPT and Google’s checkout in AI Mode add items to a cart and complete the purchase, usually after you approve it. On Forter’s network, agentic orders at the end of the latest 90 days were 10.6× their level in its first week (Aug 28 – Nov 25, 2026).

### Which AI shopping agent places the most orders?

Over the last 7 days (Nov 19 – Nov 25, 2026), by share of the agentic orders on Forter’s network: Muse 52%, Grok 21%, ChatGPT 18%, Instinct 5%, ChatGPT Agent 2% and Google 2%.

### Can ChatGPT buy things for you?

Yes, in two ways Forter sees: through OpenAI’s Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP), launched with Instant Checkout in September 2025, and through ChatGPT’s agent, which drives a store’s own pages. In March 2026 OpenAI said it would let merchants use their own checkout experiences while it focuses on product discovery.

### Can Google’s AI check out for me?

Google’s checkout in AI Mode and the Gemini app runs on the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), which Google launched in January 2026, at stores that have integrated it.

### How do AI shopping agents pay?

Scraper agents pay through the store’s own checkout, as a person would; Muse, for one, pays with Link by Stripe, which generates a one-time-use card. Protocol agents pass a payment token through the protocol: the Shared Payment Tokens ACP uses are scoped to one merchant and basket total.

## About the data

Orders Forter's network identified as placed by AI agents, aggregated and anonymized across Forter's global network of merchants and refreshed daily. Shares are of agentic orders, or of agentic spend, over the latest 90 days the data has (Aug 28 – Nov 25, 2026) unless a sentence says the last 7 days; order values are in US dollars.

Protocol agents (ChatGPT and Google) check out through commerce protocols and are identified by where the shopper came from; scraper agents (Muse, Instinct, Grok and ChatGPT Agent) drive the store's own checkout pages and are identified by the agent platform. ChatGPT Agent is counted apart from ChatGPT's protocol checkout, and "all agents" counts each agentic order once. Instinct and Grok are counted from September 1, 2026 and Muse from September 21, the days Forter started detecting them reliably, which may be later than they started placing orders; growth since the first week includes them arriving.
