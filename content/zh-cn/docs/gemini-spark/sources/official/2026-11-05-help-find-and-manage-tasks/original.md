You can find tasks you’ve assigned to Gemini in the side bar with your chats. In the side bar, you can:

- **Check the progress of all your tasks** in a high level view.
- **Pin, rename, and delete task threads** so the important ones are easy to find.
- **Filter & search for task threads** to quickly find tasks, especially ones that need your attention.
- **Add them to notebooks** to organize your projects in a dedicated, focused space.

**Important:**

- For now, assigning tasks to Gemini isn’t available to everyone. [Check the availability of tasks in Gemini](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507#reqs).
- Tasks are only listed in the side bar if you have [Keep Activity](https://myactivity.google.com/product/gemini?utm_source=help) on.

## Find your recent task threads & their progress

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
   - On a Mac, you can also open the Gemini app ![](images/img-01-4a5c21.png).
2. On the sidebar, check under “All” for your recent task threads.
3. Next to your task threads, check the icon for the progress of your tasks.
   - ![](images/img-02-b441ff.png) Ready
   - ![](images/img-03-9a79d0.png) Needs attention
   - ![](images/img-04-342154.png) In progress
4. Click a thread to open it.
   - To review the steps Gemini completed, at the top of the thread, click the current progress chip.

### Filter or search for your tasks

- **Filter your threads by status:** next to "All," click Filter ![](images/img-05-c80f31.png) ![and then](images/img-06-bfcdbc.png) the status.
- **Search your threads:** at the top, click Search ![](images/img-07-0348f6.png), then start entering keywords.

## Pin, unpin, or rename a task thread

In the Gemini web app

On the sidebar, find the thread, then:

- **Pin a thread:** hover over a thread and click More ![](images/img-08-53995d.png) ![and then](images/img-06-bfcdbc.png) **Pin** ![](images/img-09-e4e0e5.png).
- **Unpin a thread:** on the thread, hover over the Pin ![](images/img-09-e4e0e5.png) and click More ![](images/img-08-53995d.png) ![and then](images/img-06-bfcdbc.png) **Unpin** ![](images/img-10-84cc5e.png).
- **Rename a thread:** hover over a thread and click More ![](images/img-08-53995d.png) ![and then](images/img-06-bfcdbc.png) **Rename** ![](images/img-11-402a32.png).
  - If the thread is pinned, first hover over the Pin ![](images/img-09-e4e0e5.png).

In the Gemini app on Mac

On the sidebar, right-click the thread, then choose an action:

- **Pin** ![](images/img-09-e4e0e5.png).
- **Unpin** ![](images/img-10-84cc5e.png).
- **Rename** ![](images/img-11-402a32.png).

## Add a task thread to a notebook

In the Gemini web app

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. On the sidebar, hover over the thread you want to add to a notebook.
3. Click More ![](images/img-08-53995d.png) ![and then](images/img-06-bfcdbc.png) **Add to notebook** ![Notebook](images/img-12-cd71d2.png).
   - If the thread is pinned, first hover over the Pin ![](images/img-09-e4e0e5.png).
4. Click the notebook you want to add the thread to.

In the Gemini app on Mac

1. On your Mac, open the Gemini app ![](images/img-13-d2c18f.png).
2. On the sidebar, right-click the thread you want to add to a notebook.
3. Select **Add to notebook** ![Notebook](images/img-12-cd71d2.png) ![and then](images/img-06-bfcdbc.png) the notebook you want to add the thread to.

## Delete a task thread

In the Gemini web app

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. On the sidebar, hover over the thread you want to delete.
3. Click More ![](images/img-08-53995d.png) ![and then](images/img-06-bfcdbc.png) **Delete** ![](images/img-14-23ae4d.png).
   - If the thread is pinned, first hover over the Pin ![](images/img-09-e4e0e5.png).

In the Gemini app on Mac

1. On your Mac, open the Gemini app ![](images/img-01-4a5c21.png).
2. On the sidebar, right-click the thread you want to delete.
3. Click **Delete** ![](images/img-14-23ae4d.png).

## What happens when a task thread is deleted

**Important:** To manage Gemini Apps Activity, you need to sign in to Gemini Apps. [Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278668).

#### If you delete a task thread, it also deletes the following:

- The task.
- The related activity from your Gemini Apps Activity.
- Any schedules associated with that task.

#### It does not delete:

- Any files that were created or modified like files in Google Workspace.
- Any remote browser data or remote code execution data associated with the task. [Learn how to delete this remote data in Task settings](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507#delete_data).

**Tip:** If task data in Gemini Apps Activity is deleted, then that task’s activity is deleted from Gemini Apps and any associated schedules will also be deleted from the Scheduled page.
