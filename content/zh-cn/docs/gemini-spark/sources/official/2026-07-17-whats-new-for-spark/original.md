Stay up to date with the latest improvements, feature releases, and fixes for Gemini Spark. We’ll update this article with key updates as we release them, helping you get the most out of your experience.

## July 2026

### July 17, 2026

- **Get started with Gemini Spark:** You can now ask Gemini Spark to help you set up your first skills and tasks. [Learn more](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507#gs_spark).

### July 16, 2026

- **Access for US Pro subscribers:** We’re rolling out Gemini Spark in English to Google AI Pro subscribers in the US. [Learn how to upgrade](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14517446).

### July 14, 2026

- **Expanded country & language support:** We’re rolling out Gemini Spark in all Gemini Apps languages to Google AI Ultra subscribers wherever Gemini Apps are supported, except in the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom. [Learn how to upgrade](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14517446).
- **More [Google Workspace actions](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507#workspace_actions):** Gemini Spark can now help you:
  - Edit private spreadsheets and presentations.
  - Read comments on spreadsheets and presentations.
  - Edit shared documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.
  - Add images to documents and presentations.
  - Edit and refine documents, including shared ones, using the [Canvas panel](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507#other_gemini_features).
- **Remote control for Mac:** You can now ask the Gemini app to take action on your Mac from another device. [Learn how to control the Gemini app on Mac remotely](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17208717#remote_control)

### July 13, 2026

- **Smarter notifications:** We've enhanced notifications with more details whenever input is needed, and will skip sending mobile notifications for a task if you're actively present in that task’s thread in the Gemini web app.
- **Smarter sourcing:** For complex tasks that involve information from multiple sources, Spark can now retrieve and review sources in parallel for faster processing.

### July 7, 2026

- **Third-party Connected Apps:** We've started to roll out support for Dropbox and Zillow Connected Apps in Gemini Spark.

### July 1, 2026

- **Third-party Connected Apps:** We've started to roll out support for Canva, Instacart, and OpenTable Connected Apps to work with Gemini Spark.

## June 2026

### June 30, 2026

- **Topic monitoring:** Gemini Spark schedules are now better at monitoring topics across news, finance, sports, local events, and much more. [Learn more about schedules in Gemini Spark](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094710).
- **Gemini app for Mac:** Gemini Spark is now available with Schedules and Skills in the Gemini app for Mac.

### June 29, 2026

- **Google Tasks & Google Keep:** Gemini Spark can now connect to your content in Google Keep and Google Tasks. [Learn about the supported actions](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507#workspace_actions).
- **Comments in Google Docs files:** Gemini Spark can now read comments in your Google Docs files.
- **Custom Connected Apps:** Connect your personal or third-party apps to Gemini Spark with their Model Context Protocol (MCP) server URLs. [Learn how to connect custom apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17209137).

### June 17, 2026

- **Expanded availability:** Gemini Spark starts rolling out everywhere Gemini Apps are supported, except in Australia, Canada, the European Economic Area, Hong Kong, India, Japan, Nigeria, South Korea, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.

## May 2026

### May 19, 2026

- **Initial launch:** Gemini Spark starts rolling out with [Schedules](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094710) and [Skills](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094296) in the United States. [Learn more about Gemini Spark](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507).
