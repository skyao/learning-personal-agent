You can assign tasks to Gemini to handle complex, multi-stage workflows with your Google Photos. You can manage, enhance, and share your visual memories with a single task prompt.

You can use Gemini to complete a combination of actions in a single task, including:

- Automatically search, organize, and curate your photos and videos.
- Edit, enhance, and stylize your images, or create new collages and videos.
- Generate summaries of events, extract text from images, and create visual stories.
- Run workflows across Connected Apps using info from your photos.
- Set up recurring tasks to manage your photos in the background.

**Important:** We’re gradually making Google Photos available in tasks in Gemini, so it might not be available to you just yet.

## What you need

To create tasks for Google Photos workflows, you must:

- **Be 18 or over.**
- **Have access to tasks in Gemini Apps.** [Learn more about requirements for tasks](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507).
- **Connect Google Photos to Gemini Apps.** [Learn how to connect Google Photos](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15734842).

**Important:** For now, the Google Photos features available for tasks in Gemini are:

- Available [wherever the Google Photos connected app is supported](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15734842#gp_countries). This feature isn’t available in some United States regions.
- Only available in the Gemini mobile app and on the web at [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).

## Manage multi-stage Google Photos workflows with Gemini

1. On your phone or tablet, open the Gemini mobile app ![](images/img-01-4a5c21.png) or go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. Above the text box, click **Task**.
3. In the text box, tell Gemini what you want it to do with your photos or videos.
   - For example: `Find the best 15 photos from our trip to Hawaii last week. Enhance the beach shots, put them into a shared album called 'Aloha 2026', and draft an email with the link.`
   - To schedule the task, tell Gemini the time or event after which to start the task. [Learn more about schedules](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094710).
   - To specify an existing skill to use, enter `@`, then select the skill. [Learn more about skills](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094296).
4. Tap Submit ![](images/img-02-c4d89f.png).

Example prompts

Here are some examples of multi-stage workflows you can ask Gemini to perform with Google Photos:

- Find the best photos and videos from my recent block party and put everything into a new shared album called 'Summer Block Party'. Draft an email to the neighborhood group with the link.
- Brighten up my beach shots from my recent vacation and add them to a new album called ‘Beach Therapy’.
- Check my photo of the local summer concert series and cross-reference it with my Google Calendar availability. Which night can I make it to the concert?
- Every weekend, find my top photos of food, build a collage, and add it to a new album called 'What my camera ate this week'.

## What you can do with Google Photos in your tasks in Gemini

In tasks you assign to Gemini, you can perform a wide range of actions with your Google Photos.

- **Search & curate:** Find specific photos and videos by subject, location, date, or event. Ask Gemini for the best shots and to filter duplicates.
- **Edit & create:** Quickly enhance photo quality, apply quick fixes, and generate collages or stylized images. Any edits create a new copy that’s saved in Google Photos.
- **Create albums & organize:** Create new shared or private albums, and save curated photos to them.
- **Share albums:** Add or send links to shared albums in Connected Apps like Gmail, Google Docs, and Messages.
- **Schedule automations:** Set up recurring tasks to manage your Google Photos workflows, like automatically creating monthly highlight albums or sorting photos of receipts.
- **Extract text from images:** Find specific images and extract text from them. Then summarize or reformat the information in a Google Document, or ask Gemini to complete a task using that information.

**Tip:** You can ask Gemini, "What Google Photos skills do you have?"

## How Gemini works with Google Photos for tasks

- **Original photos are untouched.** Gemini doesn’t edit or overwrite your original photos. To edit a photo, a copy is made and then edited.
- **You must confirm some actions.** Gemini asks for your permission before performing actions like creating shared albums or sending emails.
- **New albums are private by default.** When Gemini creates a new album, it’s private to you. Gemini asks for your permission before sharing an album.
