You can assign Gemini tasks to purchase and complete payments for you based on conditions you set. For example, you can ask Gemini to:

- Order your usual lunch takeout from a restaurant for pickup at a specific time.
- Buy a pair of tickets for your preferred seating tier for a show at the end of the month.
- Book a nonstop flight for a specific route and date, balancing your preferred departure time and deals.
- Book a refundable room near a specific location given details like number of guests, reservation dates, and bed size.

## What you need

To use Gemini for purchases and payments, you must:

- **Be 18 or over and in the US.**
- **Sign in to the Gemini app with a personal Google Account.** For now, this feature isn’t available if you sign in with a work or school Google Account. [Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278668).
- **Have a payment method saved to your Google Wallet.** If you don’t already, you can add one when you set up a purchase task.
- **Have [History](https://myactivity.google.com/product/gemini) on.**

**Important:** For now, purchases and payments with Gemini are:

- Only available in English.
- Only available in the Gemini mobile app, the Gemini app on Mac, the Gemini app on Windows, and the Gemini web app at [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).

## Ask Gemini to make a purchase or payment

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) or open the Gemini app ![](images/img-01-d2c18f.png).
2. Below the text box, click **Task**.
3. Tell Gemini what you want it to buy or pay for.
   - For purchasing products, include specific product details, your preferred merchants, and your budget.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions.
   - If not already granted, Gemini needs your permission to use a browser to work on your task.
5. Verify or update the purchase details, like:
   - The item or list of items to purchase.
   - Merchants that Gemini can browse and buy from.
   - Your max budget, up to $1000.
   - The task expiration date, within 14 days. If Gemini doesn’t complete the purchase by this date, the task is cancelled.
   - Your payment method. If you don’t have one saved, you’ll need to add one.
   - Your shipping address.
6. Turn **Purchase confirmation** on or off.
   - By default, “Purchase confirmation” is on so you get notified to confirm the transaction before Gemini completes it.
   - If you turn it off, Gemini automatically completes the transaction when your conditions are met, without notifying you to confirm first.
7. When you’re ready to submit the request, click **Set up purchase**.
8. Follow any on-screen instructions.

**Tips:**

- On your bank statement, transactions completed by Gemini may show up with additional descriptors to help you identify them easily.
- When Gemini completes a purchase, it may use a secure virtual card number to keep your actual card details private. [Learn more about virtual cards](https://support.google.com/googlepay/answer/11234179).

### If Purchase confirmation is turned on

If you kept “Purchase confirmation” turned on, check regularly for a notification from Gemini that your purchase is ready. When your purchase is ready:

1. Select the notification to open the purchase order.
2. Review your order details.
3. To complete the purchase, select **Pay with G Pay**.

## Find & manage your purchase tasks

**Important:** If you need to make a change to an existing task, you’ll need to cancel it and create a new one.

### Find your active purchase tasks

You can find your active purchase tasks on the Scheduled page in Gemini Apps and on the Google Wallet website.

In the Gemini web app

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. On the sidebar, click **Scheduled** ![](images/img-02-74e6c3.png).
3. Under “Active,” you can find your scheduled purchase tasks.

On the Google Wallet website

1. On your computer, go to [wallet.google.com](https://wallet.google.com/).
2. At the top, click Menu ![Menu](images/img-03-bd6464.png) ![and then](images/img-04-bfcdbc.png) **Agents**.
3. Under “Active tasks,” you can find your active purchase tasks.

### Cancel a purchase task

You can cancel a purchase task before Gemini completes the purchase conversationally in the thread with the purchase task.

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. On the sidebar, click the thread with the purchase task you want to cancel.
3. Tell Gemini to cancel the purchase task.

**Tip:** You can also cancel a purchase task before Gemini completes it by deleting the schedule. [Learn how to delete a schedule](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094710#delete_schedule).

### Find purchase receipts

You can find detailed receipts for purchases made by Gemini at [wallet.google.com](https://wallet.google.com/). Receipts include the merchant name, amount, date, and payment method. These receipts do not replace official receipts from the merchant.

1. On your computer, go to [wallet.google.com](http://wallet.google.com/).
2. At the top, click Menu ![Menu](images/img-03-bd6464.png) ![and then](images/img-04-bfcdbc.png) **Agents**.
3. Under “Other tasks,” click the purchase you want to review the receipt for.
