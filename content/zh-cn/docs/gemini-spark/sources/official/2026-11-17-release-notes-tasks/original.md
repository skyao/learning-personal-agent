## 2026.11.19

### Get tasks done across your Mac apps and the web with Computer use

- **What:** With Computer use in the Gemini app for Mac, Gemini can handle multi-step clicking, typing, and form-filling across the desktop apps and websites. It works in its own separate window—so you can watch the live preview, stop anytime in one click, or keep using your screen and cursor uninterrupted. You choose which apps Gemini can use, and Gemini pauses for logins or payments and may ask for confirmation before sensitive actions.

  Rolling out in English for Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra subscribers (18+ with personal Google accounts) in the U.S. Download the Gemini app for Mac at [gemini.google/desktop](https://gemini.google/desktop/).
- **Why:** Many everyday tasks span desktop apps and websites that don’t connect to each other. Computer use handles the tedious steps in the background so you can save time without giving up your screen or cursor.

## 2026.11.17

### Find Ways to Save Time and Money with Tasks in Gemini, now available free of charge

- **What:** Starting now, Tasks in Gemini is rolling out free of charge for hundreds of millions of users in the US, India, Canada, Australia, Japan, and Korea, running on our new Hydrogen 4 model. Tasks in Gemini work on your behalf in the background to tackle multi-step projects and recurring routines to save you time and energy. Whether you are scheduling a daily morning briefing across your emails and calendar, creating an itinerary for an upcoming trip, or automating repetitive research, Tasks in Gemini handle the execution from start to finish and notify you the moment your results are ready.
- **Why:** We want to make Gemini the most helpful AI assistant for everyone. By bringing Tasks in Gemini to more people at no cost, we are transforming Gemini from an assistant that simply answers your questions into one that proactively takes action—freeing you up to focus on the work that matters most.

### Checkout with Google Pay in Tasks in Gemini

- **What:** With Google Pay in Tasks in Gemini, you can ask Gemini to watch for restocks and price drops, reorder everyday household staples within your budget cap, and complete the purchase under your direction. Simply set your item and maximum price limit when starting a Task. When Gemini finds your item within budget, it stages the checkout and sends you an itemized summary to review. Nothing is purchased without your go-ahead: once you give your explicit 1-tap and device approval, Gemini finishes checkout with Google Pay so your actual card number stays separated from the merchant but you still earn your card's points, cashback, or purchase protections during checkout.

  Rolling out in English on web, Android, and iOS for users in the U.S. (18+ with personal Google accounts).
- **Why:** By bringing single-use Google Pay virtual-card checkout to Tasks in Gemini, Gemini turns “found it” into “done” to save you both time and money, while waiting for your explicit approval before any money moves.

### Hand off tasks and steer them on the go by texting Gemini

- **What:** You can now text Gemini and manage Tasks in Gemini right from Google Messages or iMessage. After a one-time phone verification at [gemini.google.com/channels](https://gemini.google.com/channels), you can text a request, snap a photo of a school flyer to add dates to your Google Calendar, or get scheduled check-ins in a private 1:1 thread. If your plans change while Gemini is working, simply reply to the text to steer the task mid-run without starting over. Gemini works under your direction and always pauses for your 1-tap confirmation before taking major actions like sending or booking.

  Rolling out in English for users in the U.S. (18+ with personal Google account, a verified U.S. phone number and an eligible carrier). Opt in at [gemini.google.com/channels](https://gemini.google.com/channels).
- **Why:** Everyday to-dos often come up when you are on the go, and switching apps slows you down. You can now text Gemini directly and hand off tasks in seconds, steer multi-step runs in real time by replying, and get timely updates without opening a separate app.

### Forward an email, get the task done

- **What:** You can now email Gemini at [gemini@google.com](mailto:gemini@google.com) to kick off Tasks in Gemini straight from your inbox. Forward a batch of school emails to add key dates to Google Calendar, spot schedule conflicts, or send over threads and PDFs to sort out medical bills and track insurance renewals. It works from Gmail, Apple Mail, or Outlook and always replies privately 1:1 to you (never “Reply-All”) with a link to review and confirm your results.

  Rolling out in English for users in the U.S. (18+ with personal Google accounts).
- **Why:** Many of your biggest weekly tasks arrive disguised as an email thread or PDF attachment. You can now hand off that busywork with a single forward from the email app you already use—saving you from downloading files, switching tabs, or copying details into spreadsheets and calendars by hand.

### Ask Gemini to call US businesses for you with Tasks in Gemini

- **What:** Calling around to check tire stock or open service bays always seems to happen right when you don’t have 15 minutes to spare or time to wait on long hold. Now, Gemini can place calls on your behalf, even dialing multiple shops at once, scheduling calls during business hours, or placing recurring calls. Compare quotes, check inventory, or negotiate bills. You can review, approve the call plan before Gemini dials, and get a crisp summary and full transcript right in chat. Gemini identifies itself as an AI assistant, won’t share payment or sensitive account details over the phone, and allows businesses to opt out at any time.
- **Why:** Getting simple answers often means making repetitive phone calls, talking during working hours, or waiting on hold. You can hand off the legwork—bringing back the exact information or confirmation you need without disrupting your day.
