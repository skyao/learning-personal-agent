You can ask Gemini to make phone calls from the web and mobile app to complete everyday tasks for you. For example, Gemini can call businesses to:

- Check holiday hours, product availability, service quotes, and other information.
- Book, confirm, and manage appointments and reservations.

## What you need

To use Gemini to make calls for you via web and mobile app, you must:

- Be 18 or over and in the United States.
- Use Gemini signed in to a personal Google Account.
- Have your device language set to English. [Learn how to change the language you use to chat with Gemini](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15984485).

## Important info about using this feature

- Gemini starts every call by disclosing that it’s an AI assistant from Google calling on a recorded line on your behalf.
- Gemini can only make phone calls to US phone numbers to Google-verified US businesses.
- Gemini cannot make automated calls to businesses in Louisiana, Indiana, Minnesota, and Montana due to state regulations.
- In Nebraska, on Sundays and holidays, Gemini can only make calls between 1 pm and 9 pm.
- By using this feature, you accept the [Google Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) and the [Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy](https://policies.google.com/ss/generative-ai-policies).

## Ask Gemini to call a business in a Gemini chat

You can create a task to ask Gemini to call a business, or multiple businesses, from the Gemini mobile app or in the web app at gemini.google.com. When Gemini calls a business for you, it calls from a Google virtual number, so carrier rates and fees don’t apply.

To ask Gemini to call a business for you:

1. Open the Gemini mobile app or go to <gemini.google.com>.
2. Under the new chat, select **Task**.
3. Ask Gemini to make a call for you. Be specific about what you want to achieve, and include any necessary info, like dates, times, preferences, and alternative options.
   - **Service quote example**: Ask the nearest dry cleaner how much they charge to hem pants.
   - **Ask about a wait list**: Call [Restaurant Name] to check how long the wait is for a party of 2, and if we can put our name down on a list.
4. Review Gemini’s summary of the task, including the number it will call and details it will share, like your name and contact info.
5. If needed, ask Gemini to update the calling task.
6. To start the call, select **Call now** or **Call when open** (if the business is closed).
   - You can also schedule the call for a later day or time.
   - If the business does not answer, Gemini can try again.

**Tip**: After the call ends, you can review Gemini’s summary of the outcome and a transcript of the call in your chat history. If you leave the Gemini app or lock your device, you’ll get a notification with a link to the outcome of the call, a call summary, and call transcript.

### Monitor and manage calls

- You can cancel scheduled calls. To do so, on the call card, select Cancel call.
- As Gemini places a call, an on-screen card will show each call’s progress.
- If the business asks for something that isn’t in your plan, Gemini will end the call and tell you what happened.

### About daily limits

There are daily limits on the number of calls Gemini can make on your behalf. If you reach this limit, you'll need to wait until the next day to ask Gemini to make more calls.

Gemini does not support certain types of calls and actions over the phone. For example:

- **Make emergency calls**: Gemini cannot call emergency services, like 911.
- **Complete financial transactions**: Gemini cannot make payments, complete financial transactions, or share sensitive financial information (like credit card numbers) over the phone.
- **Share sensitive personal information**: Gemini cannot make calls that relay sensitive personal information, such as social security numbers, financial information, passwords, or health information.
- **Make telemarketing calls**: Gemini cannot be used to sell products or services.

### Block calls from Gemini

If your business doesn't want to get automated calls from Gemini users, you can [opt out](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7690269).

**Tip**: You can opt back in to let other users call your business with Gemini’s help on the same [Opt out or opt in page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7690269).
