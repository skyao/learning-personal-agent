You can set up text messaging with Gemini to quickly create, track, and discuss your tasks. Tasks that you create through text message appear in the side panel as a new task thread in Gemini Apps.

## What you need to text with Gemini

To set up text messaging with Gemini, you must:

- Have a personal Google Account based in the US.
- Have English set as your [Google Account language](https://myaccount.google.com/language).
- Have access to tasks in Gemini Apps. [Learn more about requirements for tasks](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507).
- Have a US phone number with an eligible mobile carrier.

To text Gemini on an Android phone, you must also:

- Have Google Messages installed. [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.messaging).
- Have RCS chats turned on in Google Messages settings. [Learn how to turn it on](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/7189714).
- Use the same phone number for Gemini and Google Messages.
- Sign in to the same Google Account in Gemini Apps and Google Messages.

To text Gemini on an iPhone, you must also have RCS Messaging and RCS Business Messages turned on in Messages settings. [Learn how to turn it on](#ios_rcs_settings).

Turn on RCS Messaging & RCS Business Messages on your iPhone

1. On your iPhone, open the Settings app.
2. Tap **Apps** ![and then](images/img-01-bfcdbc.png) **Messages** ![and then](images/img-01-bfcdbc.png) **RCS Messaging**.
3. Turn on **RCS Messaging**.
4. Turn on **RCS Business Messages**.

## Before you text with Gemini

Texts with Gemini are not end-to-end-encrypted and may be accessible by your carrier, subject to their privacy policy and terms. Deleting your Gemini Apps Activity does not delete chats from your messaging app or carrier.

## Set up text messaging with Gemini

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. At the bottom, click Settings ![](images/img-02-a69c1b.png) ![and then](images/img-01-bfcdbc.png) **Channels** ![](images/img-03-828384.png).
3. Next to “Text with Gemini,” click **Set up**.
4. Select the phone number you want to use for sending and receiving text messages with Gemini or click **Add a phone number**.
5. If needed, verify your phone number.
6. Follow any additional on-screen instructions.

After you set up text messaging with Gemini, you’ll get a text from Gemini. You can create a task, get status updates, or discuss an existing one in your text chat with Gemini.

## About attachment limits in texts to Gemini

You can include attachments like multimedia, files, and location information in your task-related texts to Gemini. Each attachment has a 15 MB limit.

## Remove your phone number from Gemini

To stop Gemini text communications, remove your number in Channels settings.

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. At the bottom, click Settings ![](images/img-02-a69c1b.png) ![and then](images/img-01-bfcdbc.png) **Channels** ![](images/img-03-828384.png).
3. On the “Text with Gemini” card, next to the phone number you want to remove, click Remove ![](images/img-04-f02acc.png) ![and then](images/img-01-bfcdbc.png) **Remove**.

**Tip:** You can opt-out or opt-in to text messages from Gemini directly in your messaging app. In your text chat with Gemini, enter “STOP” to opt-out or “START” to opt back in.

## What happens when you delete a text with Gemini

- If you delete the text chat with Gemini, it does not delete it from your Gemini Apps activity. [Learn how to manage your Gemini Apps activity](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892).
- If you delete activity from [Gemini Apps Activity](https://myactivity.google.com/product/gemini), it does not delete the text message or chat from your messaging app or carrier.

## Fix a problem

I got a text message asking me to sign in to my Google Account

If you get a text message from Gemini to sign in to your Google Account, make sure you sign in to the same account in both Gemini Apps and Google Messages.

I got a text message asking me to verify my number

You might get a text message from Gemini to reverify your number if it detects a new device or SIM.

To verify your number:

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. At the bottom, click Settings ![](images/img-02-a69c1b.png) ![and then](images/img-01-bfcdbc.png) **Channels** ![](images/img-03-828384.png).
3. On the “Text with Gemini” card, click the phone number you need to verify.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions.

I got an error that numbers from my carrier aren’t supported

For now, text messaging with Gemini is not supported on some mobile carriers.

I deleted my text chat with Gemini. Can Gemini send a new text?

If you deleted your text chat with Gemini from your messaging app, you can have Gemini start a new text chat in Channels settings.

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. At the bottom, click Settings ![](images/img-02-a69c1b.png) ![and then](images/img-01-bfcdbc.png) **Channels** ![](images/img-03-828384.png).
3. On the “Text with Gemini” card, click **Send text**.
