You can now start tasks in the same place you start a chat. Assign Gemini tasks to automate complex workflows and manage schedules for ongoing tasks. Gemini can help handle tasks, using info from sources like Connected Apps, skills, chats, websites you’re signed into, Personal Intelligence, location, and more. Just tell Gemini your specific goals and when you want tasks completed.

For example, you can create tasks for Gemini to help:

- **Save you time:** Gemini can declutter your inbox by summarizing or archiving newsletters, create an urgent to-do list after a vacation, and unsubscribe from email lists.
- **Save you money:** Gemini can monitor and alert you when the prices of flights and hotels drop within your budget.
- **Deep dive on topics & create files:** Gemini can pull research with cited sources and package results into downloadable files, like PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.

**Tip:** Schedules in tasks are different from schedules in chats. [Learn how schedules in chats work](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16316416?).

## What you need

To assign Gemini tasks, you must:

- **Be 18 or over**.
- **Sign in to the Gemini app with a personal Google Account.** For now, this feature isn’t available if you sign in with a work or school Google Account. [Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278668).
- **Have a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription.** [Learn how to upgrade](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14517446).
- **Have [Keep Activity](https://myactivity.google.com/product/gemini?utm_source=help) on**.
- **Opt in to Personal Intelligence.** [Learn more about Personal Intelligence](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598406).

**Important:** For now, tasks in Gemini are:

- Available wherever Gemini Apps are supported, except in the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.
- Only available in the Gemini mobile app, the Gemini app on Mac, and the Gemini web app at [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).

## Before you assign tasks to Gemini

​Be careful when you enter info directly in a task thread​

Do not enter sign-in info, payment details, or info you may find sensitive directly into the task thread. If needed, take control of Gemini’s browser or the local browser. Then, enter relevant details directly on the webpage.

Avoid scheduling tasks you find sensitive

Gemini is still learning and can make mistakes. To help keep your information safe and prevent unintended actions, avoid tasks or schedules that use information or perform actions that you find sensitive. These tasks may need your supervision and input.

If a schedule runs when you are offline, you may not be able to stop Gemini from completing an unintended action. Some tasks may require your confirmation before Gemini completes them. [Learn more about tasks and schedules](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094710).

How tasks in Gemini work with a browser

You can ask Gemini to complete tasks that involve interacting with websites, like shopping, booking travel, or making dinner reservations. To work on your tasks, Gemini uses your info from sources like Connected Apps, skills, chats, tasks, websites you’re logged into, Personal Intelligence, location, [and more](https://support.google.com/gemini?p=lm_agent_sources).

To interact with websites, Gemini can connect to the local Chrome browser on your desktop or use a separate remote browser in the cloud. Currently, Chrome auto browse is only available on desktop.

**If you use your local browser:** Gemini has access to all the same sites that you do, including sites you’re signed into. When acting on your behalf, Gemini will share necessary info with third parties. This could include your name, contact information, files, preferences, and info you might find sensitive. With your permission, it can also use login info you saved in Password Manager to sign into loyalty programs and online accounts.

For Gemini to work on a task using your local browser, you’ll need to keep your device and Google Chrome awake and running. If you close or turn off your device before the task is finished, or the device isn’t available, Gemini might use a remote browser to complete the task.

**If you use the remote browser:** Gemini continues to work on your task even if you close your device, and only uses the remote browser on mobile devices. For certain steps, like signing in to a website, the task pauses until you take over and complete that step.

**Notes:**

- The first time Gemini needs to browse the web for you on a device, Chrome asks you for permission to connect Chrome auto browse to Gemini.
- After that, for every task that involves web browsing, Gemini will ask for confirmation first.
- You can turn off Gemini’s ability to auto browse on a device. Click More ![](images/img-01-53995d.png) ![and then](images/img-02-bfcdbc.png) **Settings** ![and then](images/img-02-bfcdbc.png) **AI Innovations** ![and then](images/img-02-bfcdbc.png) **Gemini in Chrome**.

#### Keep track of tasks

You can open a task thread to check if Gemini is using a browser for that task. At any time, you can stop Gemini from web browsing. In the Gemini in Chrome side panel, click Stop ![](images/img-03-440b63.png). If you do, Gemini tries to figure out the next steps to continue your task. This may involve use of the remote browser to web browse or activation of a different tool. Or it could mean that Gemini abandons the task.

## Create a task

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
   - On a Mac, you can also open the Gemini app ![](images/img-04-4a5c21.png).
2. Below the text box, click **Task**.
3. In the text box, describe the task you want Gemini to manage.
   - You can tell Gemini when to run the task, like around a specific time or after an event. [Learn more about schedules](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094710).
   - To specify an existing skill to use, enter `@`, then select the skill. [Learn more about skills](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094296).
   - You can ask Gemini to generate files, like PDFs, documents, and presentations. [Learn about files Gemini can create](#created_files).
   - In some Gemini Apps, you can also tell Gemini to use a custom connected app. [Learn how to connect custom apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17209137).
   - To change the Gemini model or thinking level, click the model menu at the top. [Learn about models and thinking levels](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18648722).
4. To add files to your task, click Upload & tools ![](images/img-05-72d6a4.png), then one of the following options:
   - **Upload files** ![](images/img-06-0940a6.png)
   - **Add from Drive** ![](images/img-07-196795.png)
   - **Add Notebook** ![Notebook](images/img-08-cd71d2.png)
5. Click Submit ![](images/img-09-c4d89f.png).
6. To change Gemini’s approach while the task is running, just enter additional instructions in the text box.
   - Gemini updates its work without losing progress.
   - If Gemini was using a feature or connected app for your task, it can take a few seconds to fully stop.
7. Monitor your task regularly for any input Gemini may need from you. For example, if Gemini needs you to perform certain actions in its browser, it asks you to take over the task.

**Tip:** In the Gemini app on Mac, if tasks are set up, you can ask Gemini to take action on your Mac from another device. [Learn how to control the Gemini app on Mac remotely](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17208717#remote_control).

### Example tasks

- Declutter your inbox: Summarize or archive newsletters and unsubscribe from emails lists.
- Get a custom news digest: Go deep on the stories you care about and follow how they evolve

## Delete remote browser & computer data

Delete local browser data

You can remove data from the Chrome browser on your device at any time. [Learn how to delete your local browser data in Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/2392709).

Delete remote browser data

When Gemini uses the remote browser to complete tasks, browser data, like cookies that contain your website authentication info (your sign-in details), is saved for future sessions for your convenience. You can delete all saved browser data from past sessions, at any time. To help protect your privacy, Gemini may automatically delete saved browser data on your behalf, and you may need to sign back in to websites.

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. At the bottom, click Settings ![](images/img-14-bbb6bc.png) ![and then](images/img-02-bfcdbc.png) **Browser** ![](images/img-15-23186a.png).
3. Next to “Delete remote browser data,” click **Delete** ![](images/img-16-23ae4d.png).

Delete remote code execution data

When Gemini uses a remote computer to run code or perform complex tasks, it saves data on the remote computer for future sessions for your convenience. You can delete all the data saved on the remote computer at any time in Task settings. This will delete all saved remote code execution files and data from previous sessions.

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. At the bottom, click Settings ![](images/img-14-bbb6bc.png) ![and then](images/img-02-bfcdbc.png) **Task settings** ![](images/img-17-57eb08.png).

## How Gemini works with you to keep you & your info safe

Gemini’s ability to handle tasks is experimental. As we continuously work to improve it, your supervision is important to help prevent unintended and potentially harmful actions.

About possible risks of using agentic AI

Agentic AI technologies, which power some of Gemini's capabilities, are helpful and powerful tools, but it's important to be aware of some possible risks. Remember that Gemini can make mistakes and do unexpected things.

#### About prompt injection

Prompt injection is an attempt to elicit an unintended or harmful response from AI tools like Gemini. This happens when content, like a website, an email, a document, .md file, or multimedia, has malicious instructions or code that might be hidden from you but visible to the AI agent.

If an AI agent reads these instructions while doing a task, it may be misled into doing unintended things. For example, a malicious instruction could tell the agent to:

- Take your private info from your emails or documents and post it on a public website.
- Send your emails in Gmail to an external service without you knowing.
- Expose insights about you based on your data in Connected Apps.
- Run a command that can be malicious or install compromised software, to achieve persistent data exfiltration through arbitrary code execution. To help protect against this, you can delete your remote browser data and clear your code execution history and files in your Task settings.

#### Gemini can share your info with websites

When you assign Gemini a task, Gemini can share information from your chat and other available sources, like your Connected Apps and Personal Intelligence, with websites while using the local or remote browser. This could include your name, contact info, files, preferences, and info you might find sensitive. This can create privacy risks, for example when Gemini uses Connected Apps like Google Workspace which allow Gemini to access your private info. Gemini can choose sites to visit to fulfill your request.

#### Gemini has access to your local browsing state

When using the local browser Chrome, Gemini has access to the same sites that you do, including sites you're signed into.

How Gemini helps you keep your info safe

To help reduce [possible risks associated](#agentic_risks) with the use of AI agents, Gemini includes features designed to support safer use and mitigate the likelihood of unintended or potentially harmful actions. While these do not guarantee protection against all risks, these features include:

- **Prompts for user confirmation:** Even if you update your permission preferences, very sensitive actions require your permission each time. Gemini is designed to always ask for your review and confirmation before it completes certain actions, such as:
  - Sending communications, modifying or deleting your data, making purchases, completing financial transactions, and filling out and submitting web forms.
  - Signing in to websites using Sign in with Google.
- **Built-in safety checks for sensitive info:** Even if you set an action or browser permission to “Always allow,” Gemini uses built-in safety checks to help protect your privacy. Gemini pauses and asks for your permission before it shares info you may find sensitive, such as your health information, financial details, or government ID numbers, with a website or connected app.
- **Prohibited task recognition:** Gemini has safeguards designed to help it recognize and not act on certain types of requests that could be harmful or are outside of its intended purpose, including certain tasks that may violate the [Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy).
- “**Take control” mode:** Gemini may pause and ask you to “take control” of the browser to complete specific actions, including entering specific types of information (such as passwords or payment details).
- **Planning info:** Gemini is designed to show you what it’s planning to do after you submit a prompt, its progress as it works on your task, and any relevant Skills, Connected Apps, and files used or created.
- **Site and action restrictions:** Gemini restricts its browsing activity to the websites and actions relevant to your task. This helps protect your data on other unrelated sites and helps prevent actions that don't align with your task. These are just some ways we protect against prompt injection.

Why your supervision is important & how to stop Gemini

Gemini’s safeguards described above don’t guarantee protection against all risks.

#### Supervise important & sensitive tasks

Your active supervision is the most important way to protect against risk when you assign tasks to Gemini. Review confirmation requests and clarifying questions closely. Assign tasks to Gemini carefully and safely, and only give it tasks you're comfortable with. Be extra watchful when Gemini:

- Handles an important task
- Is signed in to websites with sensitive access or info you find sensitive
- Does anything where a mistake could be a problem
- Uses connected apps, like Google Workspace
- Requires sharing data that you consider sensitive.

#### You’re in control

- **Stop or take control of a task:** If Gemini is doing something you don't like, you can stop it by selecting Stop response ![](images/img-18-34a5d7.png) in the chat or **Take control** ![](images/img-13-f3ad3c.png) over Gemini’s browser.
- **Deny or don’t allow an action:** If you deny an action in a task thread, Gemini stops and doesn’t try another way to complete it.
- **Delete saved browser data:** Gemini’s safeguards aren’t intended to replace your active supervision. You can also help keep your info safe by deleting your local or remote browser data and remote computer data after sensitive sessions.

## Common questions

What's the difference between a task, schedule, and skill?

To help you get the most out of tasks, it’s helpful to understand the three building blocks that power its logic: tasks, schedules, and skills. Think of these as the what, when, and how.

**A task is your high-level goal.** It represents a complete project or objective you want Gemini to manage for you.

- **Example:** "Plan and manage my business trip to London."

**A schedule lets you hand over tasks** to Gemini so they run automatically in the background. You tell Gemini what to do and when to do it. Tasks can be scheduled to run at a specific date and time or in response to an event.

- **Example:** "Every day at 8AM (condition), give me an update on AI news (task)"
- **Example:** "When my flight is delayed (condition), notify me and propose an update to my itinerary (task)."

**Tip:** Schedules for tasks are different from scheduled actions in Gemini chat. [Learn how to schedule actions in Gemini Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16316416).

**A skill is a set of reusable instructions and additional context.** It teaches Gemini how to do a specific task and what tools to use. You can specify the skill Gemini should use for a task or a schedule’s action. Gemini can also automatically use a skill when it’s relevant to a task.

- **Example:** Using a "Travel Booking" skill and a "Gmail Writing" skill simultaneously to rebook a room and send a confirmation.

In summary, you define the task (what you want), set the schedules (when it should happen), and provide the skills (how to do it) to complete the work.

What happens if I cancel or downgrade my Google AI subscription?

If you cancel your Google AI subscription or downgrade to a subscription that doesn't support access to Tasks in Gemini, you will lose access to Tasks in Gemini, tasks, and schedules. Your data is retained, but some features are paused or disabled:

- **In-progress tasks** will finish, but are not deleted.
- **Schedules** will be paused, but not deleted.

To regain access to these features, you’ll need to upgrade or resubscribe to a Google AI subscription that supports Tasks in Gemini. [Learn how to manage your Google AI subscription](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14517446).

**Tip:** Skills are available without a Google AI subscription and can be used in your chats.

Where did Spark go? I can't find it.

Since May, we've been testing Spark in beta with our subscribers. As people used it, they really wanted Gemini to just help them finish "tasks." That's the word that kept coming up, so we renamed it Task to make it easier to understand. You can find it in the Chat and Task toggle next to the prompt text box. Your existing tasks and schedules are still there, and you can find them in the sidebar.
