You can set up email with Gemini to quickly create your tasks from your inbox. Tasks that you create through email will appear in the side panel as a new task thread in Gemini Apps.

## What you need to email with Gemini

To email with Gemini, you must:

- Be eligible and have access to tasks in Gemini. [Learn more about tasks in Gemini](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507).
- Have a personal Google Account based in the US.
- Sign in to the same personal Google Account in Gemini Apps and Gmail.
- Turn on Email with Gemini.
- Email Gemini at [gemini@google.com](mailto:gemini@google.com) using one of these methods:
  - The Gmail app or [gmail.com](https://gmail.com)
  - An email app with the ‘Sign in with Google’ option, like Apple Mail or Microsoft Outlook, set up with your personal Gmail account.
  - Apps that use the official Gmail API.

## Turn Email with Gemini on or off

1. On your computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. At the bottom, click Settings ![](images/img-01-a69c1b.png) ![and then](images/img-02-bfcdbc.png) **Channels** ![](images/img-03-828384.png).
3. Next to “Email with Gemini,” click **Turn on** or **Turn off**.
4. Follow any on-screen instructions.

After you turn on “Email with Gemini,” you can [email Gemini](mailto:gemini@google.com) to create a task. Once the task is created, you’ll get a reply with a link to the task.

## What’s not supported in emails to Gemini

- **Over 10 email attachments:** Gemini won’t create a task if the email has more than 10 attachments. Inline images count toward this limit, but Google Drive links do not.
- **Status updates and discussions:** For now, only task creation is supported over email. If you email Gemini to discuss or ask for an update on a task, it creates a new task and you’ll get a reply with a link to the new task.
- **Unsupported emails:**
  - Emails auto-forwarded to gemini@google.com.
  - Emails where gemini@google.com is not in the “To” field.
  - Emails sent from a Gmail account by a delegate, a non-Gmail account, or a work or school account.
  - Emails sent to modified versions of gemini@google.com, like gemini+urgent@google.com.

## What happens when you delete an email with Gemini

- If you delete an email with Gemini, it does not delete it from your Gemini history. [Learn how to manage your Gemini history](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892).
- If you delete activity from [Gemini History](https://myactivity.google.com/product/gemini), it does not delete the email from Gmail.

## About your location data

When you create tasks over email, Gemini uses a combination of location signals to provide relevant responses to location and time zone-related queries. This includes:

- Your saved home and work addresses from your Google Account.
- Your recent activity from Search services only if it’s connected in [your Connected Apps settings](https://gemini.google.com/apps).
