Muse is a personal AI agent from Meta that helps people get things done. Learn more about Muse.

Your Muse can have its own @muse.ai email address. For information about connecting Gmail, Outlook, or another inbox to Muse, learn about how Muse works with Connectors.

## Using your Muse's email address

Your Muse's @muse.ai address is separate from your personal email account. Setting it up doesn't give your Muse access to your Gmail, Outlook, or other inbox.

You can forward selected emails to your Muse to share information, or ask it to help with tasks by email. For example, you could ask your Muse to contact a restaurant about a reservation, coordinate plans, or follow up on a refund.

## Sending an email

To have your Muse send an email for you:

1. Ask your Muse to set up an email address then follow the instructions in the Muse app.
2. Tell your Muse who you want to email and what you want it to do. For example: "Email this restaurant and ask whether they have a table for four on Friday at 7 p.m."
3. When Muse asks for approval, review the email details. You can allow or deny the action.
4. If something doesn't look right, deny the action and tell your Muse in Chat what it should change.

You can review sent and received emails in Muse and ask about the progress of your task.

Emails sent from your Muse's @muse.ai address come from your agent. If you want an email to come from your personal account instead, tell your Muse which connected account to use. Learn more about how Muse works with Connectors.

## Verifying your external email addresses

You can add an email address you own and verify it with Muse. Adding your address lets Muse recognize instructions sent from that address as coming from you. It does not give Muse access to your inbox. You choose which emails to send or forward to Muse, and Muse's existing permissions still apply.

After verifying your external email address, you can forward emails or send instructions from that address to your Muse's @muse.ai address without switching to the Muse app. For example, you might forward a newsletter with an event invitation and tell your Muse to "RSVP for this event and add it to my calendar."

Only add an email account that you own and that only you can access. Anyone with access to that account can send your Muse instructions. You can manage your linked external email addresses in Muse Settings. Unlink an address immediately if you lose access or your email provider tells you your account may not be secure.

Muse treats email with extra care. It may ask you to confirm requests and refuse to action requests about sensitive topics over email, including requests about financial information, health information and credentials.

Learn more about using an email address safely with Muse Mail.

## Guidance and permissions

Give your Muse clear instructions about what it should do and what information it should share. For example: "Ask about availability, but don't confirm a booking or share my phone number."

Muse may ask for your approval before taking an action. Review permission requests carefully and only allow actions you're comfortable with. Learn more about how Muse works with your guidance and approval.

## Change your Muse's sender name

You can change your Muse's sender name in its email settings. This is the name recipients see when your Muse sends them an email. Review it before sending, especially if it includes your name or other information about you.

## Deleting emails and managing your data

## Deleting an email

You can delete individual emails from your Muse's inbox.

After you delete an email, your Muse may still remember information it learned from that email. You can also ask your Muse to forget a topic or information it has learned. Forgetting works by asking Muse to look for information about a specific person, topic or matter in its memories and supporting files and remove it to the best of its ability.

Deleting an email from Muse does not remove copies held by other email recipients.

## Deleting your Muse's email address, resetting Muse, or deleting your account

If you delete your Muse's email address, reset Muse, or delete your account, you'll lose access to your Muse's @muse.ai address. Emails sent and received through that address will be deleted, and new emails sent to it will be undeliverable.

Learn more about how to manage your Muse data.

## Receiving emails from Muse

If you receive an email from an @muse.ai address, someone has asked their Muse agent to contact you or added their Muse to an email conversation. You can reply using your usual email account and don't need a Muse account.

Emails genuinely sent with Muse use an @muse.ai address. Check the full email address, especially when the sender name looks familiar. If you receive an unwanted or suspicious email, block the sender through your email provider and report the message.

## How Meta uses information from Muse emails

When you email an @muse.ai address, Meta collects and processes your message and any attachments to provide the service and carry out tasks. The Muse user can view emails sent to their Muse. Information from these emails may become part of the user's interactions with Muse and may be used to develop and improve our AI models, depending on the user's settings and as described in the Muse Privacy Policy.

Information about you may also be shared with Muse when another person forwards an email, includes quoted email history, or includes your information in a message or attachment. This can happen even if you haven't emailed Muse directly or received an email from Muse. Learn more at Information for people who don't use Muse.

## Things to keep in mind

- Your Muse may be inaccurate or take unexpected actions. Monitor its activity and intervene as needed.
- Emails you forward may include information about other people. Review the message, quoted history, and attachments before sharing them with Muse.
- Information your Muse sends may be read, saved, or forwarded by recipients. Only ask your Muse to share information you're comfortable sending.
- You cannot use Muse to harass, stalk, scam, or pursue criminal activity. See the Muse Terms of Service. Meta may limit or disable Muse email features when it detects spam, scams, harassment or other misuse.
- Sending limits apply to emails from your Muse's @muse.ai address. If you reach a limit, Muse may be unable to send additional emails.
- Your Muse's email address is intended for individual communication and delegated tasks, such as coordinating plans or following up on an order. It isn't intended for bulk email campaigns.
