Muse Adds Try On to Show How Clothes Look Before You Buy

Meta's Muse now offers to show shoppers how clothing looks on them while they shop, with a new Try On feature announced by Alexandr Wang and the Muse account.

Muse shoppers can now see how clothes look on them before they buy. Meta Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang [announced the Try On feature on X](https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/2123138025366704149), writing: “you can now try on clothes in muse before you buy them.” Muse will offer to show shoppers how they look in an item while they are shopping for it.

The Muse account [posted its own announcement](https://x.com/Muse/status/2123143277708988897) shortly after, calling Try On “the fit check before the fit check.”

## Shopping Is Already a Standout Use for Muse

Try On is in shopping, the use Meta has singled out in Muse’s early growth. On Meta’s third-quarter earnings call in October, Mark Zuckerberg said [Muse had passed 10 million users, with shopping as its standout use](https://www.allthingsmuse.com/articles/muse-10m-users-earnings-call/), and that shoppers were spending more than $200 on average per purchase through the agent. Muse can already complete purchases for users, including through [Shop Pay checkout on Shopify stores](https://www.allthingsmuse.com/articles/shopify-muse-shop-pay-checkout/).

Try On adds a visual check before that purchase. Muse could already find an item and check out, and it now offers a preview at the point shoppers usually hesitate over size, fit, and appearance.
