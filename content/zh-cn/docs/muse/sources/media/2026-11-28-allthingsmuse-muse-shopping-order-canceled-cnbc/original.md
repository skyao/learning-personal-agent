CNBC reports a Muse order for windshield wipers was canceled 17 hours after it was placed, and finds Amazon, eBay, United Airlines, and Kohl's limiting or blocking shopping agents while agent purchases remain 0.4% of global e-commerce transactions.

A Muse shopping order placed with AutoZone was canceled about 17 hours later for an unspecified error, [CNBC reported on Friday](http://newcome.ca/ai-shopping-agents-from-openai-meta-google-hit-retailer-blocks/), in a wider look at how shopping agents from Meta, OpenAI, Google, and Instinct are running into retailer limits during the holiday season.

Maia Bittner, chief product officer at fintech startup Imprint, had authorized Muse to buy replacement windshield wipers from AutoZone last month. When the cancellation notice arrived, Muse told her, according to a screenshot she shared, “Honestly, the most reliable path now is you ordering on Amazon yourself.” Amazon is the retailer that blocked Muse from shopping on its platform in September, a block that is now the subject of [reported talks between Amazon and Meta about restoring access](/articles/amazon-meta-talks-muse-shopping-access/).

Bittner described a second Muse shopping result from last week. Asked to find a Jellycat octopus plushie for her children, Muse suggested one marked 75% off at a retailer she had never heard of, which appeared to have no reviews. She said she wants an agent to surface only sites that are not scams, in the same way she trusts search ads to do, and that agents are not there yet.

## A Patchwork of Permissions

CNBC found no single rule for agents across major retailers. Amazon, eBay, United Airlines, and Kohl’s limit what agents can do or block them outright. Coach and Gap allow agents to browse but not check out. Target sometimes blocks shopping agents by accident, according to an August audit by agent security company Billions Network. Walmart and Sephora have opened their sites to agents, and Walmart this week promoted [its Black Friday partnership with Muse](/articles/walmart-agent-gateway-muse-black-friday/), which lets Muse search products, build carts, and complete orders through Walmart’s Agent Gateway.

A Visa study published this month put 22 models from 12 AI labs through shopping tasks such as buying a television, a laptop, and a hair appliance. [The agents failed about 43% of the test runs](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/visa-finds-ai-models-fail-43percent-shopping-test-runs/), and in more than half of the failed runs the agent still bought a product that did not meet the customer’s identified requirements. CNBC also reported that agents often stall on login requirements at checkout and can need a person to clear a Captcha before continuing.

Retailer terms are settling responsibility for a bad purchase, with no shared standard in place. Best Buy’s terms state that it is not responsible for conduct, errors, actions, or inaction by a digital agent, and that it treats agent transactions as if the customer performed them. American Express said in April that it would protect eligible customers from charges related to AI agent error, and Stripe, whose Link wallet is integrated with Muse, protects some agent purchases and offers a refund guarantee.

## Small Volume, Growing Stakes

Agent purchases remain a small share of commerce. Bain & Co. research published this month puts agentic purchases at 0.4% of global e-commerce transactions and 0.2% of their value. Uber disclosed last month that AI agents booked 504 rides and Uber Eats orders during its busiest week in October, and DoorDash has recorded several hundred agent orders in total.

Muse is the largest consumer example in CNBC’s reporting. About a quarter of Muse’s 10 million users shop online through the agent, with an average spend of $200 per order, figures Meta chief executive Mark Zuckerberg gave in October. Forrester principal analyst Emily Pfeiffer told CNBC the mixed permissions mean a shopper using an agent will not see the full set of options, because some websites are unavailable to the agent.

Muse users shopping this weekend will find that orders complete most reliably at retailers that have built an approved route for Muse, such as Walmart. Browser-driven checkout at other stores can fail hours later with no reason given, and a cheap result from an unfamiliar retailer is worth checking before Muse is told to buy it.
