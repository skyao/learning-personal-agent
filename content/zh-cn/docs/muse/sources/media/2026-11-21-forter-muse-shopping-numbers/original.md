Meta introduced Muse, its personal AI agent, on September 8, 2026, and within weeks it was placing more orders on Forter's network than any other AI agent. Here is how much it buys, what it spends, and where and how it checks out, measured independently across Forter's global network of merchants.

- **52%** of agentic orders in the last 7 days (Nov 19 – Nov 25, 2026)
- **46%** of agentic orders and 43% of agentic spend over 90 days (Aug 28 – Nov 25, 2026)
- **229%** agentic orders month over month, the week Forter first detected Muse (Sep 21)
- **$220** Muse’s average order, against $230 for all agents

## What Muse is

Meta describes Muse as "a personal AI agent" that "doesn't just answer questions, it actually does the work". It runs on Muse Secure VM, a dedicated secure computer with its own browser, in the Muse app or directly in WhatsApp. It can open a browser and fill out forms, and asks for approval before it makes a purchase. To pay, it checks out with Link by Stripe, whose wallet for agents generates a one-time-use card so the shopper's real card details stay hidden; Meta says Shop Pay is coming soon. Muse is rolling out in the US on iOS, Android and muse.ai, free for most of what people need, with subscription plans for more ([Meta, Sep 8, 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)).

## How much Muse buys

Muse placed 52% of the agentic orders on Forter's network in the last 7 days (Nov 19 – Nov 25, 2026), more than any other agent.

Over the latest 90 days (Aug 28 – Nov 25, 2026) it placed 46% of agentic orders and 43% of agentic spend, though Forter has only detected its orders since September 21.

All the agents over the last 7 days: Muse 52%, Grok 21%, ChatGPT 18%, Instinct 5%, ChatGPT Agent 2% and Google 2%.

From November 23, Forter's [live Black Friday and Cyber Monday data](https://ai.forter.com/peak-season) shows Muse's share of this holiday season's agentic orders.

## The Muse effect

The week Forter first detected Muse, agentic orders on its network were up 229% on a month earlier.

By the end of the latest 90 days, agentic orders were 10.6× their level in its first week (Aug 28 – Nov 25, 2026).

## How Muse checks out

Muse is a scraper agent: it loads a store's own pages, fills in its forms and clicks through its checkout, the way a person does, so it can buy at stores with no integration. That makes it sensitive to what trips people up too, pages that change and forms that confuse, and to bot protection that challenges every automated browser.

Agents like it, shopping through stores' own pages, placed 70% of agentic orders over the latest 90 days (Aug 28 – Nov 25, 2026).

## What Muse spends per order

Muse's average order is $220, 5% below all agents’ ($230, Aug 28 – Nov 25, 2026).

All agents' average order by industry: travel $513, physical goods $241, digital goods $135 and food delivery $120.

## Where Muse's orders come from

92% of Muse's orders land in North America, against 88% for all agents (Aug 28 – Nov 25, 2026).

## Retailers and Muse

Meta announced partnerships with retailers and other businesses on September 23, and Mark Zuckerberg said Meta "will take a small fee on transactions" ([CNBC, Oct 3, 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/03/meta-muse-shopping-ai-agent.html)).

## What Muse means for your store

- ** Muse buys through your pages,** so the checkout your customers use is the one Muse uses: make product details, prices and stock readable on the page, and keep the checkout steps predictable.
- ** Don't turn Muse's shoppers away with the bots.** Blanket challenges on automated browsers stop agents acting for real customers; judge the identity and intent behind the order instead ([AI agent fraud](https://ai.forter.com/ai-agent-fraud)).
- ** Expect new card numbers.** Muse pays with one-time-use cards from Link, so a returning customer's agent order can carry a card you haven't seen before.
- ** Check your store.** The free [AI readiness check](https://ai.forter.com/ai-readiness) shows where agents get stuck.

## Questions and answers

### What is Meta’s Muse?

Muse is Meta’s personal AI agent, introduced on September 8, 2026. It works in the Muse app or in WhatsApp, runs its own secure browser, and can fill out forms and make purchases after asking for approval.

### Can Muse buy things for you?

Yes. Muse checks out on the store’s own pages once you approve the purchase, and pays with Link by Stripe, which generates a one-time-use card.

### Is Muse free?

Meta says Muse is free for most of what people need, with subscription plans for people who want to do more.

### How many online orders does Muse place?

Muse placed 52% of the agentic orders on Forter’s network in the last 7 days (Nov 19 – Nov 25, 2026).

### How can a store tell Muse from a malicious bot?

By who is behind the order rather than the browser placing it: Muse acts for a real shopper, whose identity, payment and history can be judged like any other customer’s. Blocking automated browsers outright blocks Muse’s shoppers too.

## About the data

Orders Forter's network identified as placed by AI agents, aggregated and anonymized across Forter's global network of merchants and refreshed daily. Shares are of agentic orders, or of agentic spend, over the latest 90 days the data has (Aug 28 – Nov 25, 2026) unless a sentence says the last 7 days; order values are in US dollars.

Protocol agents (ChatGPT and Google) check out through commerce protocols and are identified by where the shopper came from; scraper agents (Muse, Instinct, Grok and ChatGPT Agent) drive the store's own checkout pages and are identified by the agent platform. ChatGPT Agent is counted apart from ChatGPT's protocol checkout, and "all agents" counts each agentic order once. Instinct and Grok are counted from September 1, 2026 and Muse from September 21, the days Forter started detecting them reliably, which may be later than they started placing orders; growth since the first week includes them arriving.

Forter is independent of Meta: these are Forter's measurements of orders on its network, not Meta's figures.
