Muse's dedicated @muse.ai email address is rolling out to everyone in supported markets, ten days after it reached paid subscribers in the US and Canada. Free-plan users can now claim a handle.

Muse’s dedicated email address is rolling out to everyone in supported markets, including people on the free plan. The Muse account [announced the wider rollout on X](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/11/20/meta-muse-mail-free-canada/) on November 20: “Muse has its own email address. Grab your handle before someone else does. Rolling out to everyone in supported markets, your Muse can now send and receive emails on your behalf.”

Meta Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang posted the same update that day, inviting users to pick a muse.ai email handle. The rollout lands ten days after [Muse Mail reached paid subscribers in the US and Canada on November 10](/articles/muse-mail-available-paid-subscribers/), and nine days after [Wang said the address would be out to everyone soon, free plans included](/articles/muse-mail-free-plans-soon/).

## How Claiming an Address Works

Tap your avatar in the Muse app and head to the Mail tab, or ask Muse to set up an address. The handle a user picks becomes the part before @muse.ai, and handles are first come, first served. The address cannot be changed once it is set, only the sender name can, so the choice is permanent.

The address belongs to the agent, not to the user’s own inbox. Setting it up does not give Muse access to a Gmail, Outlook, or other personal account. Users choose which messages to forward to the address, and they can add the address to an email thread or write to it directly with a task. Users can also verify an email address they already own, so Muse recognizes instructions sent from it as coming from them, without opening the rest of that inbox to the agent.

When Muse sends mail from its own address, it asks for approval first. Users review the email details and can allow the send, deny it, or tell Muse in chat what to change. Sending limits apply to mail from @muse.ai addresses, and the address is intended for individual communication and delegated tasks, such as coordinating plans or following up on an order, not bulk campaigns. [The Academy guide to setting up a Muse email](/articles/how-to-set-up-your-muse-email/) walks through the handle and verification steps in full.

## The Last Connect Feature to Reach Everyone

Meta previewed the dedicated address at its Connect conference on September 23, alongside live voice and video with an avatar, computer use on the Mac, and access through AI glasses. Voice reached all US users on November 5, Mac computer use shipped earlier in the fall, and the email address is the last of the four to move from a limited rollout to everyone in supported markets. ITmedia AI+ reported the same November 20 expansion in Japan, where free users began reporting that they could claim addresses the same day.
