Alexandr Wang announced a dancing feature for Muse avatars: pick a dance from the share sheet, then post the clip to Instagram, Facebook, or WhatsApp, or save it to your phone.

Your Muse can dance now. Meta chief AI officer Alexandr Wang [announced the feature on X](https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/2117722403996569768), showing how a Muse avatar picks up a dance routine and shares the clip straight to social apps.

The flow starts in a chat with your Muse. Tap your Muse avatar, then the share button at the top right, and a “Share my avatar” sheet opens with a Dances tab. Pick a song and the avatar dances to it. In Wang’s demo, his own Muse, Euler, a white fluffy dog in a dark green coat, dances through five routines: “Cupid Shuffle” by Cupid, “Training Season” by Dua Lipa, “Babydoll” by Jamie Miller, “Mi Chico” by DJ Goja, and “Million Dollar Baby” by Tommy Richman.

## Posting to Instagram, WhatsApp, and Facebook

Once the dance is playing, the share row underneath offers Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook Feed, Facebook Story, and Instagram Story, or you can save the clip to your phone. The demo ends inside Instagram’s Story editor, with the dancing clip and a “Meet My Muse” card inviting viewers to add their own Muse. The demo has drawn more than 115,000 views since Wang posted it.

The avatar-sharing option [surfaced as an unreleased feature in Muse’s web app in late October](/articles/muse-avatar-sharing-web/), and the dance flow is now live in the app.

Avatars have become a bigger part of Muse in the past week. [Muse Voice, which opened real-time voice and video chats to everyone in the US](/articles/muse-voice-video-chat-us-rollout/), also puts the avatar on screen while you talk.

Wang posted another dancing clip four days later, with the caption “pov: training everyone’s muse to dance before our launch this week.” The tease named no product or date, and the launch itself is still unannounced.
