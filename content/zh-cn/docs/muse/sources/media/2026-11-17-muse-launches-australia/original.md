Meta’s personal AI agent Muse is now available in Australia, its sixth market and its first in Oceania. Meta [announced the launch](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/muse-is-now-available-in-australia/) in its newsroom, and Meta chief AI officer Alexandr Wang [announced it on X](https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/2122762964419870792), writing “g’day, australia!! muse just arrived down under.” The Muse account [posted its own announcement](https://x.com/Muse/status/2122755564866502661): “Muse, brekkie, and beach, in that order. Muse has arrived in Australia: muse.ai.” Its announcement video shows the Muse character standing in a grassy field with two kangaroos under eucalyptus trees.

Australia follows the United States on September 8, Canada on September 18, [Japan on October 23](/articles/muse-launches-japan/), [Singapore on October 27](/articles/muse-launches-singapore/), and [Mexico on November 5](/articles/muse-launches-mexico/). The launch also puts Australia ahead of the United Kingdom. Reports in early November said Meta was [preparing to launch Muse in the UK within weeks](/articles/muse-uk-launch-within-weeks/), which would have made the UK the sixth market and the first in Europe.



## What Launches in Australia

Muse is available in Australia on iOS, Android, and the web at muse.ai, and in a Mac app, with support for Meta’s AI glasses coming soon. The service is free, with subscription plans for heavier use. Australian tech outlet EFTM, which used Muse for a week before the launch, [reports the paid tiers at $31 and $149 a month](https://eftm.com/2026/11/meta-muse-ai-agent-launches-today-in-australia-my-muse-lloyd-is-already-hard-at-work-283490) in Australian pricing.

At launch, Australians can connect Muse to Gmail, Google Calendar, Instagram, and OpenTable, covering email, schedules, saved content, and restaurant reservations. A set of local connectors will roll out progressively: Canva for design, Domain and realestate.com.au for property listings, SWEAT for fitness, Kogan.com for electronics and appliances, MECCA for beauty products, Officeworks for school and office supplies, Ticketek for event tickets, and ShopBack for price comparisons and cashback on flights, hotels, and shopping. The Canva connector is also available to Muse users worldwide.

Two recent features are part of the Australian launch. Real-time voice conversations, which [rolled out to everyone in the US on November 5](/articles/muse-voice-video-chat-us-rollout/), work on mobile, web, and Mac, and [Spaces](/articles/muse-spaces-custom-experiences/), the custom experiences users build inside Muse by describing an idea, are available as well.

## Control Stays With the User

Muse runs on Muse Secure VM, a dedicated computer in the cloud that stores the data and credentials for connected services while keeping passwords and payment methods out of the agent’s view. Users choose what their Muse can access, and Muse checks with them before sensitive actions such as sending an email or making a purchase.

Will Easton, Meta’s managing director for Australia and New Zealand, said: “With the festive season coming up, the timing couldn’t be better. From finding and buying the perfect gift to planning and booking a holiday, Muse is designed to take work off Australians’ plates.”

The Australian launch keeps Meta’s pace of roughly one new market every two weeks since early October, after the first month confined Muse to the United States and Canada.
