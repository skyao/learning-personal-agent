Alexandr Wang says Muse Mail, the dedicated email address that reached paid subscribers in the US and Canada on November 10, will be out to everyone soon, including free plans.



Meta Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang [posted on X](https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/2120612306904346637) on November 11 that Muse Mail, the dedicated email address for Muse, which [reached paid subscribers in the US and Canada on November 10](https://www.allthingsmuse.com/articles/muse-mail-available-paid-subscribers/), will be “out to everyone soon including free plans.”

Wang’s post quoted his own November 10 launch post. Paid subscribers, he wrote, should reserve their Muse.ai email handle while they can.

## A Paid Launch, Then a Wider One

Muse Mail gives a user’s Muse an email address of its own. Other people can write to the agent directly, and users can forward items to the address or add it to an email thread for Muse to handle. [The Academy guide to setting up a Muse email](https://www.allthingsmuse.com/articles/how-to-set-up-your-muse-email/) walks through the handle and verification steps.

The November 10 rollout covered paid plans only. Wang’s post extends the expected audience to free plans, but free users cannot claim an address yet. Wang gave no date for the free rollout, and he did not say whether the expansion will go beyond the US and Canada, where Muse Mail is currently available.

## What to Do Now

Paid subscribers in the US and Canada can claim their handle today, ahead of the wider rollout Wang described. The setup runs through the avatar and mail tab in the app, or by asking Muse directly.
