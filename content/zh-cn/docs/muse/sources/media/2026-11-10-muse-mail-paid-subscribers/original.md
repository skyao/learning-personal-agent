Muse now has its own email address. Paid subscribers in the US and Canada can have their Muse send and receive emails on their behalf. [The Muse account announced on X](https://x.com/Muse/status/2120201720651563286): “Muse has its own email address! Your Muse can now send and receive emails on your behalf, available now for paid subscribers in US and Canada.” Meta Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang [posted the same news](https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/2120200628312129858) shortly before, saying subscribers can forward items to the address or add it to a thread.

The feature had been arriving in waves. This rollout does not include free-tier users.

## How to Get Started

In a follow-up post, [the Muse account said](https://x.com/Muse/status/2120201723394527673) subscribers can tap their avatar, open the mail tab, or ask their Muse directly. They can then add the address to a thread or forward an email to it.

Users had already seen a similar setup flow in the app. A card headed “Give your Muse its own email” offered a Setup button, and Muse confirmed the mailbox was live after setup. [The setup steps are covered in the Academy guide](https://www.allthingsmuse.com/articles/how-to-set-up-your-muse-email/).

## From Preview to Paid Availability

Meta previewed the address at its Connect conference on September 23, alongside live voice and video with an avatar, computer use on the Mac, and access through AI glasses. Of the four capabilities announced that day, the address is the last to reach formal availability. Voice [reached all US users on November 5](https://www.allthingsmuse.com/articles/muse-voice-video-chat-us-rollout/), Mac computer use shipped earlier, and glasses access has not launched yet.

## What the Address Is For

The address belongs to the agent. The Gmail and Outlook connectors connect Muse to accounts the user already owns. Other people can write to Muse directly through Muse Mail, and users can forward items for the agent to handle without passing them through a personal inbox.
