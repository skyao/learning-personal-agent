[Meta](https://www.meta.com/) plans to price its soon-to-be-released pocket-sized artificial intelligence (AI) device, [Muse Charm](https://www.meta.com/muse-charm/), at $300 and offer buyers a free 5G data plan for up to 12 months, The Information [reported](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/meta-bets-300-muse-charm-boost-ai-hardware-business) Thursday (Nov. 19), citing an unnamed source.

In addition, Meta delayed the launch of its smartwatch, which had been targeted for this year, to 2027 so that the company can focus on Charm, according to the report.

Asked by The Information about these claims, Meta spokesperson [Tyler Yee](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tyler-yee-963a1451) said, per the report: “We’re charmed by the interest in our AI devices, and we’ll have more to share soon.”

Meta CEO [Mark Zuckerberg](https://www.meta.com/about/leadership/mark-zuckerberg/) announced Muse Charm in September during his keynote at the [Meta Connect](https://www.meta.com/connect/) event. Zuckerberg described Muse Charm as a small, [handheld device](https://www.pymnts.com/facebook-meta/2026/meta-readies-pocket-sized-ai-device-december-launch/) that fits on a keychain and lets the user talk to Meta’s personal AI agent, [Muse](https://ai.meta.com/muse/). He added that Meta was finalizing the product and planned to have it ready to ship in December.

“You just go ahead and tap here in the fingerprint sensor in the corner, and you can start talking without having to unlock a phone or open an app,” Zuckerberg said while holding a prototype of the device. “So, we’ve packed a lot of technology into this little guy. So, if you’re not wearing glasses, this is going to be by far the fastest way to talk to your Muse and to show it what’s going on around you.”

PYMNTS reported at the time that Meta was the first big platform to ship a [device built for an agent](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/meta-bets-tamagotchi-style-device-can-sell-personal-ai/). Until Zuckerberg’s announcement of Muse Charm, Meta’s focus was on [AI-enabled glasses](https://www.pymnts.com/facebook-meta/2026/meta-and-essilorluxottica-bet-on-299-smart-glasses/).

It was reported in May that Meta was [expanding its wearables effort](https://www.pymnts.com/facebook-meta/2026/meta-plans-ai-pendant-as-part-of-wider-wearables-effort/), was planning to grow its AI glasses lineup and had set a goal of selling 10 million wearable devices in the second half of the year. Meta’s strategy is to promote more use of its AI models and products, such as subscription versions of its apps and consumer AI agent.

Two months earlier, in March, PYMNTS reported that Meta aimed to [share what it learns](https://www.pymnts.com/facebook-meta/2026/meta-aims-to-share-brick-and-mortar-success-stories-with-wearables-retailers/) at its Meta Lab stores with retailers of its wearables.
