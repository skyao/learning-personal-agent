Gopuff and Meta have launched a direct Muse connector that lets users order more than 5,000 groceries and essentials in chat, with delivery in as fast as 15 minutes.

Muse users can now order groceries and household essentials through Gopuff without leaving the chat. [Gopuff and Meta announced the direct integration](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261124395632/en/Now-Muse-Can-Do-the-Grocery-Run-With-Gopuff-Delivering-in-Minutes). Muse finds what a user needs at the nearest Gopuff, builds the order, and checks out once the user approves it. Orders are fulfilled by Gopuff and delivered in as fast as 15 minutes.

The connector gives Muse access to more than 5,000 products, including fresh groceries and household essentials. It plugs directly into Gopuff’s own platform, so the live inventory, pricing, promotions, and delivery times Muse shows come from the same systems that run the Gopuff app.

## How Ordering Through Muse Works

Users link a Gopuff account in the Muse app under Settings, then Connectors, or by typing “Connect to Gopuff” in a chat and signing in to Gopuff or creating an account. Once connected, a user can ask in plain language, and Gopuff’s examples include turning a saved Instagram recipe reel into a shopping list for four, restocking coffee and oat milk using remembered brand preferences, and finding an open location for a midnight ice cream order.

Before anything is purchased, Muse shows what is in stock at the nearest Gopuff and when it will arrive, then asks for approval. Customers can also ask Muse for an order status update to track a delivery in the chat. [Fox Business covered the launch](https://foxbusiness.com/fox-news-food-drink/metas-muse-can-now-shop-groceries-through-gopuff-deliver-minutes).

## What Gopuff Brings to the Connector

Gopuff owns its micro-fulfillment centers, product assortment, and local delivery network. The company reports a 99.5% order accuracy rate and 24/7 service across that network.

“Earlier this year, we brought AI-native shopping to the Gopuff app with the launch of Go. Integrating directly into Muse marks another important milestone in our agentic commerce strategy,” Alberto Menolascina, chief business officer at Gopuff, said in the announcement. “Millions of people can now get groceries and everyday essentials delivered in minutes through Muse, simply by asking.”

Menolascina said Gopuff’s vertically integrated model delivers orders with no middlemen, no markups, and no substitutions. Muse users who are new to Gopuff can use a welcome offer that includes free delivery on their first three orders, as well as access to exclusive deals.

## Grocery Ordering Is Becoming a Connector Contest

Gopuff is the latest grocery and retail name to connect directly to Muse. In Canada, [Loblaw became Muse’s first Canadian retail partner](https://www.allthingsmuse.com/articles/loblaw-muse-canada-shopping-voice/) on November 18, letting shoppers build a basket and check out in Muse. In the UK, [the Muse launch earlier today](https://www.allthingsmuse.com/articles/muse-launches-uk/) included Sainsbury’s and Ocado Retail among its grocery partners. In the United States, [Walmart opened its Agent Gateway to Muse](https://www.allthingsmuse.com/articles/walmart-agent-gateway-muse-black-friday/) for Black Friday shopping this week, with shoppers approving purchases through Agent Profiles inside their Walmart accounts.

The Gopuff connector differs from those supermarket integrations in its delivery window. Walmart, Loblaw, and Sainsbury’s orders run through larger grocery operations, while Gopuff fulfills from its own nearby micro-fulfillment centers, which is how it offers delivery in as fast as 15 minutes. Users can connect Gopuff at muse.ai or through the Muse app.
