## The October 23 sentence, read closely

The standing record for all three partners is one sentence in Meta's Japanese newsroom post of October 23, 2026, the day Muse launched in Japan. In that post, Meta said that, along with the launch of Muse in Japan, it would proceed with integrations with Mercari, operator of the flea market app, with Money Forward ME, and with Ticket Pia.

Read that wording at its actual weight. It is prospective. It names three services and says integration work will proceed. It gives no date, and it does not say any of the three was live on launch day. Money Forward ME is the proof of why the wording matters: all three names sit in the same sentence, but only one of the three has since produced its own release saying an integration has begun. Being named by Meta on October 23 and being live in Muse are two different states, and Japan now has one partner in each column to show it.

## What Money Forward ME actually does in Muse

Money Forward's release of October 26, 2026 gives the working detail for the one live connector, and it is more specific than the launch coverage was.

With the user's permission, Muse can use Money Forward ME data on income, expenses, and assets inside a conversation. The release gives three uses. First, review recent spending and sort out subscriptions that are candidates for review. Second, build a savings plan toward a goal such as a trip, and track progress against it. Third, summarize the shape of assets held across banks and brokerages, where a pattern can be hard to see account by account. Connection is made from the connector screen in Muse settings, or from chat.

The limits are in the same release, and they belong in this post. The connection is read only: Muse cannot change or update Money Forward ME data through it, and no trading, withdrawal, or transfer can be made through the integration. The login credentials for the banks and services a user has linked inside Money Forward ME are not shared with Muse. The user can disconnect at any time. Money Forward also states plainly that Muse's answers are information and planning support, not a substitute for professional financial or investment advice.

Caution

Do not let the one live connector do work it has not done. Money Forward ME being live says nothing about Mercari or Ticket Pia, which are separate companies with separate integrations. It also says nothing about what a Mercari connector would do if it arrives: buying, selling, listing, and payments in a marketplace raise different questions from a read only finance feed, and no source checked for this post describes a Mercari feature set. When a launch release exists, this site will describe that release. Until then, the Mercari column is a name and a Meta sentence from October 23, and nothing more.
