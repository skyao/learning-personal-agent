## The short version: voice, text, and dictation, with your phone nearby

The confirmed core is small, and it is worth keeping it that size. A Meta post announced the launch. A trade report with a named reporter, TechCrunch's Aisha Malik on November 10, 2026 at 7:29 AM PST, filled in how interaction works: voice calls, text messages, or dictation directly on the smartwatch.

The avatar is part of that confirmed core. TechCrunch reports that users can personalize their Muse avatar, into a person, an animal, or another type of character. The Gadgeteer, a secondary source, describes the character as front and center in the watch app. The personalization fact is TechCrunch's. The placement detail is The Gadgeteer's, and this post keeps that split visible.

The download figure in TechCrunch's piece gives the launch its context, and it needs its label too. TechCrunch reports that Muse became one of just 12 mobile apps to pass 10 million U.S. downloads within their first 40 days in the App Store. The Gadgeteer attributes the same figure to Sensor Tower estimates. An estimate carried by two reports is still an estimate, and this post prints it as reported, not as a Meta count.

## What rests on secondary reports: tasks, connectors, and the watch face

This is the section readers will want to overread, so the sourcing comes first. No Meta page checked on November 10 or November 11 lists which tasks or connectors work from the wrist. The detail below is secondary, from two outlets that published their own write ups on November 10, and it could change when Meta publishes its own help page.

The Gadgeteer, November 10, 2026, read in full, lists notifications, shortcuts and a watch face, and tasks and connectors from the wrist. On the watch face point, it describes a Muse complication that puts Muse one tap away. Gadgets and Wearables, November 10, 2026, read in full in the November 11 checks, goes one step further on tasks. A user can start a task, read an update, or approve an action from the wrist, without pulling out the phone. Muse then continues work through connected services. Its examples include searching for movie tickets and spotting a scheduling conflict between a meeting and a dentist appointment, then carrying out the task once approved.

Read at that weight, the picture is plausible and limited at the same time. Starting a task and approving an action from the wrist fits what a companion app does. It does not prove that every connector Muse offers on the phone works on the watch, that a booking completes from the wrist without the phone, or that the full connector list is available on both watch platforms. No source checked for this post names a connector by name as wrist ready. Until a Meta page does, the honest sentence is the narrow one: two secondary outlets report tasks and connectors from the wrist, and Meta has not confirmed the list.

Field note

The test that would settle this in five minutes is on your own wrist. Open Muse on the watch. Start one ordinary task you already run on the phone. Note three things: whether the task starts on the watch or only opens the phone app, whether a progress update appears on the watch without you asking, and whether an approval prompt can be answered from the wrist. Those three answers, with your watch model and OS version, say more than any launch post will.

## The paired phone boundary

TechCrunch states the boundary in its November 10 report: the watch app requires a paired phone and a Muse account. The Gadgeteer, secondary, puts the same point as setup: the latest Muse app on your phone comes first.

That boundary answers one question and leaves a second one open. It answers whether Muse on a watch works on its own. On this record, it does not. It leaves open whether a cellular Apple Watch can use Muse away from the paired phone, on a run or in a store with the phone left at home. The Gadgeteer says your phone can stay in your bag. That is proximity, in its own wording, not cellular independence. Gadgets and Wearables says you can act without pulling out your phone, which is also proximity. No source checked on November 10 or November 11 states the cellular case, and this post does not guess at it.

Caution

Do not read "Muse is on Wear OS" as "Muse is downloadable on every Wear OS watch today." The Gadgeteer, secondary, says the Wear OS app was waiting in the Google Play Store, wording that suggests Wear OS availability may lag the November 9 announcement. Gadgets and Wearables, also secondary, notes that Meta has not published a proper Wear OS compatibility list. TechCrunch reports the launch itself. A launch announcement, a store listing that is still waiting, and a compatibility list that does not exist yet are three different states, and Wear OS sits somewhere among them until a Google Play listing or a Meta page settles it.
