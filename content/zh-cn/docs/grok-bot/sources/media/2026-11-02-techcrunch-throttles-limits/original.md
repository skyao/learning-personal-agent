Over Halloween weekend, some Grok Bot users awoke to something especially spooky: Their weekly usage limits were unexpectedly maxed out, even if they hadn’t been using SpaceX’s agentic AI overnight. Some users reported on X that their [usage](https://x.com/MadnessOfMouth/status/2117028659420606892) jumped from as low as [50% to 100%](https://x.com/GregGaskell/status/2117083536859738409).

SpaceX CEO Elon Musk confirmed in an X post on Sunday that this was not a glitch.

“The cost of serving Grok Bot was unsustainably high relative to revenue,” he [wrote](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2116992258520064397) in a reply to a question from a user. He has not addressed the issue on his main feed but has continued boasting that Grok Bot can help you [buy gifts](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2116989604104392969) for your family.

> The cost of serving Grok Bot was unsustainably high relative to revenue
>
> — Elon Musk (@elonmusk) [November 1, 2026](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2116992258520064397?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw)

Grok Bot has been positioned as a success story for the newly public SpaceX, which said in a [filing](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/spacexs-ai-business-is-beating-expectations/) last month that AI now makes up 52% of company income. Its renamed “Super Intelligence” division actually turned a profit of $431 million, which is a rarity in the AI industry. Its profit would’ve been [higher](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/spacexs-ai-business-is-beating-expectations/) if not for $1.2 billion of one-time costs related to its acquisition of [Cursor](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/15/spacex-officially-closes-its-cursor-acquisition/). Musk recently referred to Grok Bot as having “the [fastest growth](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Qmc0IN_JuzQ) of a product that I’ve ever seen in my life.”

Musk has not yet shared how subscribers’ usage limits will be impacted moving forward.
