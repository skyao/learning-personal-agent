People are increasingly navigating their financial lives with AI every day. Now, Plaid is powering read-only financial account connectivity for [Instinct](https://instinct.com/). Instinct is a personal assistant that works where people already interact: through iMessage and WhatsApp. With Plaid, U.S. consumers can securely connect their financial data to Instinct for personalized conversations in a familiar interface. Instinct joins more than 800 AI-native startups building with Plaid.

### **What Plaid makes possible within Instinct**

Plaid paved the way for secure, consumer-permissioned data sharing in U.S. open finance. Now, we are applying the same trusted infrastructure, used by 1 in 2 U.S. consumers with a bank account, to deliver real-time financial insights to thousands of U.S. Instinct users.

Instinct is quickly emerging as an option for consumers who want a personal assistant in their pocket, with 35% of users connecting a personal credit card within three weeks. Once a user has created an Instinct account, they can ask their assistant financial questions directly in iMessage or WhatsApp. Instinct will then prompt a user to authenticate their financial accounts through Plaid for more personalized answers. From there, Instinct can read the accounts alongside other helpful connections, like email and calendar. Instinct can help track refunds, follow up on HSA reimbursements, and plan a personalized holiday budget.

Instinct reads balances and transactions to spot patterns, make recommendations, and proactively flag what needs attention. Here are a couple other scenarios:

- Splitting grocery bills with a roommate: “Help me split the last three grocery orders into two separate expenses.” Instinct finds the orders, works out who owes what, and messages each roommate their share.
- Proactively managing duplicate charges: “It looks like the hotel charged you twice, but your confirmation email only shows one night. Want me to draft the dispute?” Instinct asks for approval before taking any next steps.

### **Building a better financial future**

In addition to Instinct, Plaid is helping consumers securely connect their financial data to other AI platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Grok, Muse, and Perplexity’s Personal CFO, which recently expanded to Europe. People who have never tried a budgeting app are finding help from assistants that can understand them holistically and share tailored financial guidance. Of users who choose to connect more than one bank account to an AI platform with Plaid, 75% connect a second bank account within 1 day, and 65% do so within one hour. These users are also choosing to keep more than 95% of their connections active, indicating that they are finding daily value in their experiences.
