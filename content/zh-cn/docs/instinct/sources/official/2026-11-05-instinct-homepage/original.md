## Instinct is a personal assistant that understands what you're working on and what's important to you.

The interface is simple: there are no new interfaces. You can text or call it. It can use a phone and computer in the same way that you do.

It can order ingredients for a new recipe, book a train home for the weekend, order groceries, send flowers to a friend, or find a court for the weekend.
