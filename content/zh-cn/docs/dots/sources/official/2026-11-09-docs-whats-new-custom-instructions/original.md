### Set custom instructions for your dot

Tell your dot how you’d like it to respond and work with you. You can ask it to update your instructions in conversation or edit them in **Customize → Behavior**. [Learn how to set custom instructions](https://learn.chatgpt.com/codex/dots/controls).
